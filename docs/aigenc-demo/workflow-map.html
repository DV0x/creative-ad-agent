Creative Machines · next-gen engine · in build

One brand URL in. Six on-brand ads out.

The pipeline is an agentic workflow — a sequence of specialist steps, each one grounded in the step before it.

Generating an image was never the hard part. Getting output that traces back to the brand was. So it's built as a chain, with an independent critic before anything ships.

STEP 1

Research

Surfaces the brand's real story and the buyer's actual words — from its site, reviews, forums.
The starting truthEvery claim is sourced. Every gap is named, never invented.
→
STEP 2

The Bet · strategy

Diagnoses the one winnable want and the real reason people don't buy yet.
Grounded in researchIt can't look anything up — it can only reason from what Research sourced.
→
STEP 3

Hooks

Writes ad angles — each one extracted from a specific proof point, not a template.
Grounded in The BetEvery hook targets the exact buyer and want the strategy named.
→
STEP 4

Art Direction

Turns each hook into a concrete shot — real objects, a scene, a layout a stranger could draw.
Grounded in the hookThe picture must demonstrate that claim — not just decorate it.
→
STEP 5
the quality gate

The Critic — swap test

An independent reviewer that never saw the writer's reasoning tries to kill every ad.
The swap testStrip the logo — could a rival run this unchanged? If yes, it's killed and sent back.
→
STEP 6

Render + Vision-gate

Generates the image, then re-reads the finished picture against the plan.
Grounded in the specIf the render missed a detail, it does one targeted retry before shipping.
Each step is grounded in the one before it.
That's how creative gets on-brand by construction — not by luck.
It's also the unit you replicate: the same shape runs a performance loop, an influencer loop, a social loop. One workflow, many jobs.
