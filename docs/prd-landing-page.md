## BRIEF (paste this into Claude Design)

```
Creative Machines — Product Brief

The bet. In 24 months, D2C brands won't brief agencies for paid-social
creative. They'll hand that work to agents that do the research, not
just the rendering.

Why now. Meta and TikTok burn 20-50 ad variants a month per brand.
Agencies can't ship at that cadence. First-wave AI image tools are
fast but obvious — they skip research, miss brand, and stamp everything
with the same flat aesthetic. The window is open for a system that
does both: reads the brand like a senior copywriter, then renders
like a specialist art director.

The outcome shift.
- Before: Brief → agency or freelancer → $5K-$15K/mo → 2-week rounds
  → four ads if you're lucky → run them hopefully.
- After: Paste URL → five minutes → research-backed ads → tweak any
  artifact by chat → ship the same day.

The insight. Every AI ad tool in market prompts on the product
description. We prompt on the brand's research — their ICPs, pain
points, proof points, their own words. Hooks aren't written. They're
extracted. Every hook must trace to something the brand has already
proven, said, or sold. If you can't source it, you rewrite it. The
moat is research as a visible, editable artifact. Not a black box.

Who it's for.
- The D2C founder hunting their next $100K MRR push, burning weekends
  on Canva.
- The growth marketer at a 10-50 person brand shipping 30+ variants
  a week with no agency budget.
- The agency operator running 5-20 D2C clients, trying to 10x
  throughput without 10x'ing the team.

None of them want another prompting tutorial. They want to ask for
what they need and get it back.

What good looks like.
- First run takes under five minutes and returns research a copywriter
  would read twice.
- Every hook fails the "could a competitor copy-paste this?" test.
  The ones that survive are owned.
- A marketer opens the hooks file, rewrites one line, regenerates one
  image. Total time: under a minute.
- The output doesn't look AI. People ask who made it.

Non-goals.
- Not a design canvas (you're not Canva).
- Not video (static-first).
- Not a scheduler (bring your own).
- Not white-label (agency tier exists; the brand stays Creative Machines).

Design the landing page. Use the design system. You pick everything.
```

---

## Why this voice works

- Led with the bet and why now, not with product features
- Replaced "target audience bullets" with Tuesday-afternoon scenes of each buyer
- Added non-goals — a senior PM move that kills scope creep
- Every claim is a verifiable outcome, not a feature description
- No marketing adverbs, no "AI-powered" anywhere
- Hands Claude Design a worldview, not a checklist
