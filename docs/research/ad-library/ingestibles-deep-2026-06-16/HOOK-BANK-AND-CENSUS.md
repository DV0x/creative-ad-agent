## 2. Formats found in the wild that are NOT cleanly in the 18 (gaps the data surfaced)

The library is a good prior, but live ingestibles ads run several formats the 18 doesn't name. Per the S126 rule ("a comp-surfaced format not in the library must still be buildable"), flag these:

- **New-product / new-flavor launch** ("Introducing Hazelnut Millet Cocoa", "NEW: Protein Oatmeal", "Get your Drop") — **very common**; maps loosely to Hero (18) but the *job* is announcing newness, not a quality shot.
- **Celebrity / ambassador endorsement** — ON × RCB (cricket), AG1 × Hugh Jackman, MuscleBlaze × Ajith. Not a customer testimonial; a distinct lever.
- **Cultural / branded-content / trend-jack** — OLIPOP's reimagined song, Poppi × Love Island. US functional sodas. Loosely near Meme (4) but it's branded entertainment, not a meme.
- **Sampler / trial pack** — Blue Tokai "pick any 3", "taste-test 3 flavours". Low-commitment trial; near Offer/Bundle but a distinct job.
- **Subscription offer** — Liquid I.V. "subscribe, save 30%". A recurring-revenue mechanic, not a one-shot discount.
- *(niche: occasion/seasonal, engagement/poll "Battle of the Berries", sweepstakes "win Lollapalooza", quick-commerce "refill in 10 min")*

## 3. Hook bank (real, spend-endorsed openers)

- **Curiosity / pattern-interrupt:** "Your cravings aren't random. This straightens them out." · "It doesn't numb hunger. It calms it." · "Doing everything right but still no shift?" (OZiva) · "Struggling to switch off at night?" (Wellbeing)
- **Clean / anti-junk (signature):** "No B.S." (RXBAR) · "Drink protein, not chemicals." (TWT) · "No artificial flavour, no sweetener, no drama." (ON) · "Chemicals in your oil? Read the label." (Anveshan) · "Pure 25g protein. No junk. No bloat." (Yogabar)
- **Us-vs-them / quality:** "Cheap protein or certified clean protein?" (Bal Bharat) · "Not all protein powders are high quality." (ON) · "Hydrates better than water alone — 3× the electrolytes." (Liquid I.V.)
- **Science / proof:** "India's first liposomal Berberine — 13.6× more absorbed." (Wellbeing) · "Powered by HydraScience™, 10 clinical trials." (Liquid I.V.) · "Clinically-backed — four human trials." (AG1) · "Every batch tested. #NothingToHide." (TWT)
- **Stat-led:** "26g protein — India's highest-protein oats." (Yogabar) · "15g protein, 6g fiber, 1g sugar." (Magic Spoon) · "75 ingredients, one ritual." (AG1)
- **Problem / condition-first:** "PCOS feels like too much?" (OZiva) · "Tired of mid-workout crashes?" (MuscleBlaze) · "If your isolate feels heavy or artificial… that's the fillers talking." (TWT)
- **Honest-confession / advertorial:** "Why our whey costs more (honest truth): raw whey went ₹800→₹2,400. We don't compromise." (Bal Bharat)
- **Occasion / culture / flavor (US-heavy):** "Hydration for Dad — Father's Day." (Liquid I.V.) · "Consistency looks good on Hugh." (AG1) · "Soda's back, but better." (Poppi) · "Flavor in full bloom." (OLIPOP)

## 4. The cross-market angle split

- **India = prove the health.** Clean/anti-chemical, dose/science, problem→solution, certified-quality. Benefit-led.
- **US = sell it like a consumer brand.** RXBAR/AG1/Liquid I.V. still lead clean+science — but the functional sodas (OLIPOP, Poppi) **lead with FLAVOR, nostalgia, and culture** (music, Love Island, packaging glow-ups, celebrity), with the health benefit (prebiotic, 5g sugar) as *background reassurance*. The opposite of India's benefit-first.

Templating rule: **US functional beverage = fun-consumer-brand-first, health-second; India ingestibles = benefit-first.**

## 5. Implications for the build

1. **The 18 covers ~the bulk of what runs in ingestibles** (10 strong + 3 partial). No need to invent — adapt the workhorses: what's-inside, offer/bundle, science, stat, PAS, us-vs-them.
2. **Add the surfaced gaps** to the buildable set for this category: **new-launch/new-flavor**, **celebrity/ambassador**, **cultural/trend-jack** (US bev), **sampler/trial**, **subscription offer**.
3. **White space (founder-POV, testimonial) is unclaimed** but de-prioritized for now — revisit when we want differentiation rather than parity.
