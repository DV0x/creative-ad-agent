# Skin Library — the native-DR skin box, evidence-based (2026-06-17)

The canonical **skin** half of SKIN × ENGINE, built from real ads. A skin is the *disguise* — what an ad pretends to be so it doesn't read as an ad. An **engine** (PAS · offer · us-vs-them · proof) pours into it. Build the two separately; mix freely.

This consolidates the 7 live exemplars in `../wild-native-exemplars-2026-06-16/` with a fresh 15-ad pull (2026-06-17 — mostly fintech lead-gen: Stable Money, Ditto, Shift, smallcase + a few ingestibles/apparel). Images for the **new** ads live in this folder; the original 7 stay in the sibling.

> **Role:** this is the *evidence base*. The actual cell skin box gets **distilled** from it (thin) when we restructure the cell — this doc is not the binder, it's the quarry. **Treat as qualitative signal, not measured** — no per-format performance data exists (`../FINDINGS-2026-06-15.md`); these are revealed-by-spend, not proven by CVR. The ₹20–30k beta is the only real judge.

## The two findings that built this doc

1. **One engine → many skins (the split, proven in the wild).** Stable Money runs basically ONE pitch — *"your money sits at 3%, it should earn 8.3%"* — across **four** skins (fake Google search · tortoise-vs-cheetah · 3D cash stacks · a bank receipt). Ditto runs ONE lead-gen pitch — *kill the objection → free expert help* — across **four** (WhatsApp chat · book-page quote · event poster · handwritten sticky note). This is exactly why we split: write the pitch once, re-skin it many times.

2. **Native is a dial, not a switch.** These ads disarm at different depths, and that depth is the spine of this library. Entries run **deepest disguise → least**:
   - **Tier 1 — Disguise:** *fakes a real thing* (a chat, a search, a receipt). Reads as not-an-ad at all. Deepest — and where our native bet is strongest, because it's least like competitors' work.
   - **Tier 2 — Blend:** *borrows an organic or editorial form* (a candid photo, a routine table, a book quote, a pretty card). Reads as content, not a brand ad.
   - **Tier 3 — Constructed:** *clearly an ad, but breaks the category look* (a metaphor, a comparison, a hero face, a clean poster). Pattern-interrupt, not disguise. Parity/contrast — not our edge.

## How to read an entry (and the one rule)

Each skin has four parts:
- **Disguise** — the form it fakes or borrows.
- **Earned when** — the buyer / material / awareness that makes it honest. (A skin with no real payload underneath = brand fluff, not DR.)
- **Real example** — the ad it's drawn from + **the move to steal**.
- **The fake twin** — the generic version that wears the same clothes; forbidden.

**The one rule — adapt, never copy.** We never reuse a surface ("fake Google search for FDs"). We reuse the **move** ("stage the buyer's real search query, and win the result") and the **MAKER** refills it with this brand's real material. A copied skin is a costume any rival could wear; a derived skin is swap-proof.

## Tier 1 — Disguise (fakes a real thing · deepest native)

### chat-screenshot — "a real conversation, not an ad"
- **Disguise:** a fake DM / WhatsApp thread between the buyer and the brand.
- **Earned when:** the buyer has a real back-and-forth worry (an objection, a "how much do I need?") that a short exchange resolves → a lead-gen CTA. Needs a real question lifted from research.
- **Real example:** Ditto — buyer asks *"1Cr? 2Cr? how much cover should I take?"*, the brand answers, ends on *"Book a FREE call."* Pure UI-mimicry. `../wild-native-exemplars-2026-06-16/ditto__chat-screenshot__PAS-qa__leadgen.jpg`
- **The fake twin:** a too-clean chat with marketing-perfect bubbles and no real UI chrome; a "conversation" nobody would ever actually have.

### search-screenshot — "I googled this and here's the answer"
- **Disguise:** a fake Google (or app-store / maps) results page — often with a hand-drawn circle on the winning result.
- **Earned when:** the buyer is at a genuine comparison moment ("best place to park ₹1L?") and the brand wins the head-to-head on a hard number.
- **Real example:** Stable Money — search *"Best place to park ₹1,00,000?"*, Big Bank ₹3,000/yr vs Stable Money ₹8,300/yr circled in purple, "Verified". `./stablemoney__search-screenshot__us-vs-them-proof__leadgen.png`
- **The fake twin:** an invented-looking results page with fake competitors or numbers that don't hold up; Google chrome faked badly.

### receipt / document artifact — "the proof is the paperwork"
- **Disguise:** a real-looking statement, receipt, mini-statement, invoice, or ticket — the artifact IS the ad.
- **Earned when:** the payoff is an outcome you can *show* as a document (a payout, a saving, a result), not just claim.
- **Real example:** Stable Money — a *"MINI STATEMENT / LAST 5 TRANSACTIONS"* showing *"+₹25,000 Monthly Payout from FDs."* Show, don't tell. `./stablemoney__receipt-artifact__proof-outcome__leadgen.jpg`
- **The fake twin:** a "statement" in rounded marketing fonts with a fantasy number; a document a real bank would never print.

### handwritten note — "a real person scribbled this for me"
- **Disguise:** a handwritten sticky note / Post-it / scrawled card, held in a real hand and shot candidly in a real (slightly messy) room. The analog, warmer cousin of the digital notes-app screenshot — it nests inside a `real-world moment` (the hand + the room sell the realness).
- **Earned when:** the payload is a short, personal, one-to-one message — the buyer's exact question answered, or a small honest confession. The handwriting itself says *a human, not a brand, wrote this for you*.
- **Real example:** Ditto — a yellow Post-it in a hand on an office floor: *"Not sure which Health Plan to pick? Ask Ditto! (It's FREE)"* → "Book now · 100% Free Consultation." (added 2026-06-18) `./ditto__handwritten-note__PAS-objection-freeconsult__leadgen.png`
- **The fake twin:** a "handwritten" font on a clean digital sticky graphic; a too-perfect note floating on a gradient; a studio shot instead of a candid room. The mess (the real thumb, the carpet, the ballpoint) is the whole disguise — polish kills it.

*Carried from the frozen list but still **unseen in evidence**: the **digital notes-app screenshot** (the iOS Notes UI — its handwritten analog cousin is now evidenced, above) and **forum / Reddit screenshot** (an unbranded thread where a stranger vouches). Flagged, not fabricated.*

## Tier 2 — Blend (borrows an organic / editorial form · semi-native)

### real-world moment — "someone just posted this" · OPEN subject
- **Disguise:** a candid phone photo of a real moment. Subject is **open and derived** — a person, a hand, a **pet**, an object, a scene — the MAKER decides *who'd actually post this*.
- **Earned when:** the proof lives in a real-world moment (a customer's review, a founder's hands, the product in its use-place). **testimonial and founder-POV already live here** — built + validated.
- **Real example:** joggersports — the shorts worn mid-pull-up in a gritty subway, then a price card. `./joggersports__real-world-carousel__offer__apparel.png`
- **The fake twin:** a glossy stock-photo "candid"; the smiling-model-in-a-void; an AI-perfect scene with no grain or honest imperfection.

### relatable-routine — "your week looks like this"
- **Disguise:** a day-by-day / week table of the buyer's grind.
- **Earned when:** the problem is a repeated lived pattern that lands in one glance — PAS's native home.
- **Real example:** Shift — *"Job searching on your own in 2026 looks like this:"* a Mon–Fri table of futility → *"there's a better way."* `../wild-native-exemplars-2026-06-16/shift__relatable-routine__PAS__leadgen.png`
- **The fake twin:** a generic schedule with no real, specific, painful detail — a table that could be any brand's.

### quote / editorial card — "established wisdom, not a sales claim"
- **Disguise:** a true line set on a print surface (a book page, a newspaper column) — borrowed print authority. **Often two-zone:** disarm at the top (the quote), convert at the bottom (CTA, rating).
- **Earned when:** a single counterintuitive truth carries the sale and the brand wants gravitas, not loudness.
- **Real example:** Ditto — *"Term insurance costs less the younger you buy. And that price never changes."* in italic serif on an aged book page (an unrelated astronomy text — borrowed gravitas), red underline on the urgency line, "Book a FREE Call" below. `../wild-native-exemplars-2026-06-16/ditto__quote-card__proof-urgency__leadgen.png`
- **The fake twin:** an inspirational quote on a stock gradient; a "book page" with no real print texture; gravitas with no true insight under it.

### decorative card — "too pretty to be an ad"
- **Disguise:** a soft, designed frame (watercolor florals, Pinterest aesthetic) wrapped around a hard offer.
- **Earned when:** the offer is blunt (a price, a deadline) and needs a disarming wrapper so it doesn't read as pushy.
- **Real example:** Shift — a floral *"FINAL CALL — get your job offer for $20, ends tonight"* + a day-by-day plan. Pretty disarms; the offer converts. `../wild-native-exemplars-2026-06-16/shift__decorative-card__offer-urgency__leadgen.png`
- **The fake twin:** decoration with no payload underneath (brand fluff); a pretty card that *buries* the offer instead of setting it up.

### typographic-correction card — "watch me reframe the category"
- **Disguise:** an edit performed on screen — a strikethrough, an "Is→was", a crossed-out word in a second colour.
- **Earned when:** the move is a reframe ("X used to be true; not any more") backed by a hard proof point.
- **Real example:** Athena — *"Meeting Protein Goals Everyday ~~Is~~ was Expensive before Athena"* + *"₹60/cup · 20g."* `../wild-native-exemplars-2026-06-16/athena__strikethrough-textcard__us-vs-them-price__ingestibles.png`
- **The fake twin:** a strikethrough with no real before/after; a "correction" that reframes nothing.

## Tier 3 — Constructed (clearly an ad, breaks the category look · least native)

### metaphor poster — "an image stands in for the claim"
- **Disguise:** an object / animal / person embodies the abstract benefit.
- **Earned when:** the benefit is abstract and a single vivid image makes it instantly felt.
- **Real example:** Stable Money — a slow tortoise (savings 3%) vs a sprinting cheetah (FD 8.3%); smallcase — a person drowning in pillow + blanket for *"Fix your sleep with Fixed Deposits."* `./stablemoney__metaphor-poster__us-vs-them__leadgen.png` · `./smallcase__concept-poster__PAS-proof__leadgen.jpg`
- **The fake twin:** a clip-art metaphor that decorates rather than argues; a visual pun with no payload.

### comparison poster — "this vs that, in numbers"
- **Disguise:** a side-by-side of two options, the gap made physical (small stack vs big stack).
- **Earned when:** the sell is a clean head-to-head on a number.
- **Real example:** Stable Money — *"Where would you park your salary?"* Traditional 3% (small stack) vs FD 8.30% (big stack), premium 3D render. `./stablemoney__comparison-poster__us-vs-them-proof__leadgen.png`
- **The fake twin:** a comparison table that's all design and no real, checkable contrast.

### hero-face editorial — "premium: a face + a data callout"
- **Disguise:** a high-production hero face with a diagnostic stat overlay; editorial/luxury feel.
- **Earned when:** premium positioning + a personal diagnostic number is the hook. (More brand than native — use sparingly.)
- **Real example:** Humanedge — a man's face, *"bio age 37 vs chrono 43,"* *"Ageing is inevitable. Decline is optional."* `../wild-native-exemplars-2026-06-16/humanedge__stat-overlay__proof__premium.jpg`
- **The fake twin:** a glossy face with a meaningless number; premium gloss with no real proof.

### clean / product poster — "the honest designed ad" (the parity skin)
- **Disguise:** a well-built brand poster — product, badges, benefit, CTA. Doesn't pretend to be anything else.
- **Earned when:** table-stakes parity (an offer, an event, a feature set) where a clean ad is enough and a disguise would be dishonest. The deliberate *non*-native option.
- **Real example:** Ditto — *"Free LIVE Insurance Masterclass,"* shield + Zerodha + 4.9/5 + 50K attendees; The Whole Truth — the *"Whole Sale"* flex (person + product); Nobero — *"commute to match mode"* lifestyle callouts. `./ditto__clean-poster-event__offer-proof__leadgen.png` · `./wholetruth__person-with-product__offer-sale__ingestibles.png` · `../wild-native-exemplars-2026-06-16/nobero__lifestyle-callout-carousel__feature-offer__apparel.png`
- **The fake twin:** the category average with a logo swap — the slop the whole binder exists to beat.

## Cross-cutting moves (not skins — techniques any skin can borrow)

- **Two-zone (disarm top, convert bottom).** Many native ads earn the read with a disarming top half (the quote, the chat, the routine), then switch to explicit conversion below (CTA, rating, legal). The Ditto quote card is the clearest. Lets a skin disarm *without* giving up the hard CTA.
- **Hand-annotation.** A hand-drawn circle or underline (the purple ring on Stable Money's search result; the red underline on Ditto's quote) adds a human, organic touch and directs the eye to the one thing that matters.

## What this changes about the plan

- **The skin box is now evidence-based — and bigger than the frozen list of 7.** Newly-evidenced skins: search-screenshot · receipt/document artifact · handwritten note · metaphor poster · comparison poster · typographic-correction · the constructed tier.
- **The handwritten-note skin (Ditto, 2026-06-18) partially fills the notes-app gap** — its analog cousin is now evidenced. Still unseen: the *digital* notes-app screenshot and Reddit/forum — don't treat those two as validated.
- **Build priority follows disarm-depth.** Tier 1 (fake-a-real-thing) is where the native bet is strongest and least like competitors' work; Tier 3 is parity we may not need to build first.
- **What's already built:** testimonial + founder-POV (both `real-world moment`). Everything else is open.
