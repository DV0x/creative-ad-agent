# FROZEN — Native DR Format Kit (build blueprint)

**Date:** 2026-06-16 · **Status:** STRATEGY FROZEN — implementation starts next session.
Replaces the flat "23-format list" thinking for the cell's DR formats with a **SKIN × ENGINE** model.

## The bet
Build DR formats that **differentiate by going native** ("doesn't-look-like-an-ad"), for DR-hungry D2C founders **and** the local-services lead-gen paying track. The same kit serves **ingestibles (Track 1)** and **lead-gen (Track 3)** — 4 of our 7 live exemplars were lead-gen, so the skins travel.

## The model: SKIN × ENGINE
A native ad = a disarming **skin** wrapped around a DR **engine**. Build the two axes separately and mix freely (way more range than fixed templates, all typography/layout = our static strength, all slop-proof).

**ENGINES (the persuasion / payload):** PAS · offer · us-vs-them · proof-stat

**SKINS (the disguise):**
- **real-world / native moment** — subject is **OPEN and derived** (a person, a hand, a pet — cat/dog, an object, a scene… *anything real-world*). NOT a fixed menu. The human points (testimonial, founder-POV) are **already built**; the rest of the space (pets, objects, candid scenes) is new.
- **chat-screenshot** (fake DM / WhatsApp)
- **notes-app / text-card**
- **quote / editorial card**
- **relatable-routine** ("your week looks like this")
- **Reddit / forum screenshot**
- **clean-poster** — parity skin for the offer/science table-stakes

**Proven mixes (from the 7 live exemplars):** PAS×chat = Ditto WhatsApp · offer×decorative-card = Shift floral · us-vs-them×strikethrough-text = Athena · proof×quote-card = Ditto book-page · PAS×real-world = a 1am nightstand shot.

## Fixed guardrails (everything else is DERIVED, not menu-picked)
1. **MAKER keystone** — derive every element (subject, setting, treatment) from the copy's provenance + a named real maker. *Derive, don't list.* This is what decides the real-world skin's subject: **who would actually post this, in what real world?** (cat-treat brand → the cat; sleep supplement → the 1am nightstand; road-trip snack → the car cupholder.)
2. **DR guardrail** — the skin disarms, the payload converts. **No payload = brand fluff, not DR.** A cute dog with no offer/proof/problem doesn't qualify.
3. **Native look** — phone-shot / candid / real, not a polished studio ad.
4. **Static slice** — native-UGC is strongest in *video*; we make **statics**, so our version is the static slice (candid photo + text overlay / story-frame), not talking-head clips.
5. **IN/US dial** — same format; India turns claims/price/density **UP**, US turns them **DOWN**.
6. **Skip pure-brand emerging** (flavor-world, trend-jack, meme-for-laughs) — brand, not DR.
7. **"Revealed-winner" = budget proxy, NOT measured performance.** The ₹20–30k beta is the only real judge.

## Why this is the bet (the findings under it)
- **No audited per-format perf data exists** anywhere → revealed-winner proxy + practitioner consensus, not measured CVR.
- **IN = benefit/DR-first (67% DR); US = brand-first (48% brand).** DR is essentially the India playbook.
- **DR running:** offer (31) · catalog-DPA · us-vs-them · bundle · science · PAS. **Momentum** (recent + multi-variant, the active wave): PAS, science, us-vs-them.
- **Native is white space in ingestibles but the dominant industry trend** (post-ATT "creative is the new targeting" + ad-blindness + Reels/automation) — directional, not measured. It's typography/layout-driven (our static strength) and dodges the genericness/slop problem (native is *supposed* to look raw).
