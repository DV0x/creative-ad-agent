# Wild native-format exemplars (captured from a live IG feed, 2026-06-16)

Seven real ads the user saw in their own Instagram feed. They confirm — and extend — the finding that **native, "doesn't-look-like-an-ad" formats are where DR creative is moving.** Captured live (not from the Ad Library scrape), so treat them as a qualitative signal, not a measured one.

## The model these prove: NATIVE SKIN × DR ENGINE

A native ad = a disarming **skin** (looks like organic content / a real UI) wrapped around a hard DR **payload**. The skin earns the scroll-stop; the engine does the converting. Build the two axes separately and mix them:

- **DR engines** (the persuasion): PAS · offer · us-vs-them · proof/stat
- **Native skins** (the disguise): chat-screenshot · notes-app · quote/text-card · relatable-routine · UGC-selfie · Reddit/forum · (clean-poster, for parity)

## What they change about the plan

1. **Add the chat/conversation-screenshot skin** — strong, and we hadn't listed it.
2. **5 of 7 are text-first / fake-UI** (product barely shown) → native = typography + layout, which is our static generator's strength and dodges the AI-poster-slop problem.
3. **4 of 7 are lead-gen / services** (Shift, Ditto ×2, Humanedge) → this kit serves the **local-services paying track**, not just ingestibles (Athena). The skins travel across both.
4. **The guardrail holds:** the skin disarms, but every one carries a real DR payload (offer / proof / objection-kill). A skin with no payload = brand fluff, not DR.
