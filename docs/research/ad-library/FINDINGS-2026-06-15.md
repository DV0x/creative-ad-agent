## The three patterns that matter

### 1. The format mix tracks how much the buyer has to think

The clearest pattern across everything we pulled: a category's ad formats are set by **how big a decision the purchase is.** Three modes so far:

- **Impulse D2C — low consideration** (a ₹399 tee, a phone case → Bewakoof, Souled Store, Ringke, Cover it up). The mix is **catalog (DPA) + discount/code + product-demo + risk-reversal.** The job is "show the thing and make the deal frictionless." Almost no science, no problem-solution depth. Ringke literally runs a discount code on every ad; Souled Store puts "10% cashback on app" on all of them.
- **Trust-gated D2C — high consideration** (something you ingest → TWT, Wellbeing, OZiva, MuscleBlaze, Optimum, Bal Bharat). The mix flips to **problem→solution + science/mechanism + clean/us-vs-them + curiosity hooks.** The job is "earn belief before the buy." Dose numbers, absorption multiples, "no amino spiking," "your cravings aren't random."
- **Local service — lead-gen, not a sale** (study-abroad, hotels → Leverage Edu, Yocket, Treebo). The **offer is the chassis** (a free consultation, a discounted first booking) and the **hook is the only variable** (urgency, success-story, objection-kill, curiosity). The CTA is Book/Apply, never Shop. Yocket wraps one constant offer — "Book a FREE 1:1 consultation" — in a dozen different hooks.

**Why it's useful:** this is a routing rule. Before deriving a creative, read the buyer's consideration level and the conversion event (sale vs lead) — that already narrows which formats fit. It's an input to the cell's match→derive step (still gated). Hypothesis with three data points, not a law — widen it as we mine more categories.

### 2. Founder-POV and testimonial/review-screenshots are unclaimed white space

Across **both** D2C categories — protein and apparel/accessories — not one brand runs a founder's face or a real customer-review screenshot. They live in offers, science, problem-hooks, catalogs, and creator-UGC. (Cover it up's "10,000+ customers" is a stat, not a review; creator-UGC reels are paid creators, not the founder and not a verbatim review.)

Two categories, same gap → strong signal these two structures are an easy own. **And TWT already has both formats built** (testimonial S128, founder-POV S129). So the bet to build those first wasn't just internal taste — the competitive field is leaving them open.

### 3. A shared claim stops differentiating once everyone makes it

In protein, "clean" is now table stakes: OZiva ("Certified Clean Label, US CLP"), Bal Bharat ("Informed Certified, no amino spiking"), even Optimum ("clean whey, no drama") all claim it. **Bal Bharat is running TWT's exact play** — clean, no-amino-spiking, proof-not-claims, anti-marketing — via certifications. So TWT's defensible edge is **real-food + brand voice**, not the word "clean." General lesson: when comp shows a claim is everywhere, it's a parity feature, not a differentiator — push the angle somewhere the field isn't.

**A note on reading the proxy:** the strongest endorsement signal in the corpus is MuscleBlaze running the same Fish-Oil-+-Multivitamin combo offer for **470–597 days** (1.5+ years). When a brand keeps one creative alive that long, that's the closest thing to "this works" the Ad Library can give you. The category's evergreen workhorse is the offer/combo card.

## Per-category reads (compact)

**Protein / nutrition (D2C, trust-gated).** MuscleBlaze + Optimum own offers/combos + champions (cricket: ON×RCB, MuscleBlaze×Ajith). Wellbeing owns science-depth ("India's first," "13.6× absorbed"). OZiva owns the best hook craft (curiosity/contrarian openers) + women's health. Bal Bharat is the small challenger on TWT's clean/proof turf. White space: founder-POV, testimonial.

**Apparel / accessories (D2C, impulse — DailyObjects' orbit).** Catalog (DPA) + discount + demo + risk-reversal dominate. Cover it up's MOFT folio demo is the cleanest **"own-the-middle" product-demo** in the whole corpus. Ringke owns the **fear/PAS hook** ("your screen won't warn you before it cracks") + relentless codes. Mokobara owns **premium-craft brand voice** + "30-day free trial." White space: founder-POV, testimonial.

**Local service (lead-gen).** Education (Leverage Edu, Yocket): the offer is "free consultation," hooks carry the variety — ₹0/scholarship, deadline urgency, **success-story** ("first girl from her small town to study in UK"), objection-kill ("No GMAT required"), eligibility quiz, desi-meme. Hospitality (Treebo): discount-led (67% OFF, 19-variant winner) + bundle + scale-trust. Note: "testimonial" here mutates into a **student success-story** — same format job, different material.

## Hook bank — real, spend-endorsed openers (seed the methodology)

**Trust-gated (protein):**
- Curiosity/contrarian — "Your cravings aren't random." · "Doing everything right but still no shift? This might be why." · "It doesn't numb hunger. It calms it." (OZiva)
- Honest-price — "Why our whey costs more (honest truth)…" (Bal Bharat)
- Quality us-vs-them — "Cheap protein or certified clean protein?" · "Not all protein powders are high quality." (Bal Bharat / ON)
- Science/novelty — "India's first Triple Magnesium Complex" · "13.6× more absorbed" (Wellbeing)
- Commitment question — "Are you committed to reaching your peak fitness potential?" (MuscleBlaze)

**Impulse (apparel/accessories):**
- Fear/protection PAS — "Most Pixel 10 screens get their first scratch within 7 days. Yours is next." (Ringke)
- Demo curiosity — "This isn't a normal iPad cover. It changes how you *use* your iPad." (Cover it up)
- Pattern-interrupt promo — "Our boss is on leave today 👀 so we're doing something we're not supposed to." (Ringke)
- Premium-craft voice — "The craftsmanship of a tuxedo, tailored for your pocket." (Mokobara)
- Identity — "Oversized. Overpowered." / "Main character energy." (Bewakoof)
- Risk-reversal / bundle — "30-day free trial." (Mokobara) · "Why buy just one?" (Spigen)

**Local service (lead-gen):**
- Objection-kill — "No GMAT required." (Yocket)
- Pattern-interrupt — "We owe you an apology…" (Yocket)
- Curiosity/impossible — "Think Harvard is impossible? What if full funding is available?" (Yocket)
- Success-story — "The first girl from her small town to study in the UK." (Leverage Edu)
- Offer + scale-trust — "67% OFF + Swiggy One." · "850+ hotels in 120+ cities." (Treebo)

## What this means for the build

1. **Prioritising founder-POV + testimonial is validated by the field, not just taste** — they're unclaimed in both D2C categories. Keep them at the front of the format-scaling queue (S130).
2. **Add a consideration/conversion read to the cell's format match** — impulse-sale vs trust-gated-sale vs lead-gen routes to different format families (catalog/offer/demo · problem/science/us-vs-them · offer-chassis+hook). Feed this into the gated match→derive wiring.
3. **Testimonial mutates by buyer** — D2C = a customer using the product; local service = a success-story. The format doc should carry both skins (skeleton same, material differs).
4. **Brand-specifics:** TWT — lean real-food + voice (clean is commoditized); own founder-POV/testimonial before rivals notice. DailyObjects — product-demo ("own the middle"), device catalog, premium-craft voice, protection fear-hook are category-proven. Education beta — the success-story + objection-kill + free-consult chassis is the live playbook.

## Method + how to extend

- New category or market: edit `scripts/ad-research/config.json` (brands + `country` + buckets), `node scrape-ad-library.mjs`. Pin `page_id` to dodge the wrong-brand trap.
- Free re-use of past comp pulls: `node ingest-harness-cache.mjs`.
- New angle on an existing corpus: point a lens (see `scripts/ad-research/README.md`) at the saved `corpus.md` — no re-scrape.
- Next obvious sweeps: more local-service categories (clinics, gyms, restaurants, real-estate) to widen the consideration-level rule beyond three data points.
