# Format repertoire — living spec (23 formats)

**Status:** the living version of the format list. The original **18** were frozen in S126 (`docs/SESSION_126_*2026-06-13.md` Part 6) but only ever lived in that session doc; this file carries it forward and adds **5 formats surfaced from live Meta Ad Library comp** (ingestibles deep-dive, 2026-06-16 — see `ingestibles-deep-2026-06-16/HOOK-BANK-AND-CENSUS.md`). **18 + 5 = 23.** Should fold into the cell format library (`agent/.claude/skills/cell/references/formats/`) when the gated match→derive wiring is built; until then this is the spec.

## The frozen 18 (S126 — see that doc for full job/brand-supplies detail)

**Tier 1 — cold/native (copy-driven, need almost no brand visual world):**
1. Founder POV · 2. Notes-app / iMessage · 3. Reddit / forum screenshot · 4. Meme / tweet-style · 5. Testimonial / review screenshot

**Tier 2 — cold-to-warm (proof + information):**
6. Problem→Solution (PAS) · 7. Us-vs-them · 8. Listicle / top-reasons · 9. What's-inside / ingredients · 10. Product demo / callouts · 11. Science / mechanism · 12. Advertorial / editorial · 13. Stat / quote card · 14. Lifestyle / product-in-use · 15. Infographic

**Tier 3 — warm / retargeting:**
16. Offer / discount card · 17. Bundle / value-stack · 18. Hero product shot

> *Ingestibles census note (2026-06-16):* of these 18, **13 appear** in live ingestibles ads (10 strong + 3 partial); the 5 Tier-1 native formats are **white space** in that category (unclaimed, currently de-prioritized).

## +5 surfaced from live comp (added 2026-06-16)

Formats brands actually run that the 18 didn't name. Provenance: ingestibles ad-library deep-dive (IN + US, 452 ads / 284 concepts).

| # | Format | Job (what the ad does) | Brand supplies | Real examples | Fit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 19 | **New-launch / new-flavor announcement** | Make *newness itself* the message — "here's the new thing" | a new product/flavor/SKU + what's new | TWT "Introducing Hazelnut Millet Cocoa" · Magic Spoon "NEW: Protein Oatmeal" · Sleepy Owl Protein Coffee · OLIPOP new flavors | **Very common**; any awareness tier when there's a launch cadence. *Distinct from Hero (18): the job is news, not a quality shot.* |
| 20 | **Celebrity / ambassador** | Borrow a known person's trust | a real endorser / partnership | AG1 × Hugh Jackman · Optimum Nutrition × RCB (cricket) · MuscleBlaze × Ajith | Cold trust-building; asset-heavy (needs a real ambassador). *Distinct from Testimonial (5): not a customer.* |
| 21 | **Cultural / trend-jack** | Ride a cultural moment so the ad reads as culture, not an ad | a sharp cultural tie + production | OLIPOP reimagined "Lollipop" song · Poppi × Love Island | Cold/native, high craft; US functional-bev-heavy. *Near Meme (4) but it's branded entertainment.* |
| 22 | **Sampler / trial pack** | Lower the trial barrier — "try a few, find your favourite" | a multi-SKU starter / variety pack | Blue Tokai "pick any 3 / taste-test 5 brews" · Sleepy Owl assorted packs | Tier 2/3 conversion for taste-driven categories (coffee, soda, flavours). |
| 23 | **Subscription offer** | Convert to recurring purchase | a subscription + standing incentive | Liquid I.V. "subscribe, save 30% every order" · AG1 subscription | Tier 3 warm/retention; recurring-consumption products. *Distinct from one-shot Offer (16).* |

## Notes
- These 5 are **category-validated for ingestibles**; some may not generalize (e.g. cultural/trend-jack is strongest for US consumer-brand beverages, celebrity needs a real ambassador). Treat as buildable when comp surfaces them for a given brand, per the S126 rule: *"a comp-surfaced format not in the library must still be buildable."*
- The repertoire is a **prior**, not scripture — comp updates it per brand/category from live winners. Expect more additions as we deep-dive beauty + local-services.
