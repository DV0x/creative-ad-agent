# How people actually run static ads for performance marketing — research synthesis

**Date:** 2026-06-12 (S125, research-first session per S124 close)
**Method:** 8 parallel Perplexity sonar-pro queries (high search context, subject-binding system prompt — same settings as the mini-eval harness). Raw answers in `q1..q8-*.md`, raw JSON alongside. Total cost $0.56.
**Question:** "How are people currently running static ads for performance marketing? How do they do it and how do they come up with concepts?" — asked to inform S124's open question: *where is our pipeline going wrong?*

---

## The headline finding

**The industry does not produce the creative leap either — and it doesn't try to.** Performance statics in the wild are won by **angle-audience match expressed in deliberately boring, proven formats, at volume** — not by king-grabs-kebab-class ideas. The leap-class work the reference ads represent (Nutrich/Zepto, TWT's "behind the protein") comes from **brand-campaign agencies** (TWT's was made by Manja, a creative agency, as a brand campaign), not from the performance-statics pipeline at all. Two different industries, two different bars.

This directly answers S124 Part 6 #5 (the bar question): **no, the platform does not need the leap on every ad.** Practitioner consensus says it doesn't need it on *any* ad — it needs the right angle, a clear offer, real customer language, a proven format, and enough concept diversity for Andromeda.

---

## 1. The actual production process (q1, q2)

The standard 2025–26 workflow at performance agencies and DTC in-house teams:

1. **Research → angles** (creative strategist): mine reviews (Amazon/Trustpilot), Reddit/communities, competitor ads in Meta Ad Library / swipe tools (Foreplay, Atria). Output = voice-of-customer file + angle list. "Identify the pain point, find reviews of existing solutions, record the language, reuse that language in ads" (Valchanova's message-mining method).
2. **Brief** (strategist + media buyer): short, concrete — angle, offer, win metric (thumbstop/CTR/CPA threshold), funnel stage, format assignment, variation count. Flighted: brands should test **8–16 new concepts/month** (2–4/week).
3. **Copy first** (DR copywriter): primary texts, headlines, on-image copy from frameworks — problem/solution, us-vs-them, testimonial, cost-savings.
4. **Design** (designer in Canva/Figma off template systems, e.g. "Creative OS templates"): build base static, duplicate, change ONE thing per variant. **3–5 variants per concept** is the consensus sweet spot.
5. **Test** (media buyer): ABO for testing → CBO for scaling. **4–7 days + meaningful spend before kill decisions** (post-Andromeda; the 24–48h kill is dead).
6. **Iterate**: winners get **at most ~2 variants** (Motion strategist: "I'm capping my teams at two"); most production bandwidth goes to NEW concepts, not tweaks.

Key vocabulary: a **"concept"** in this industry = an angle expressed in a format ("testimonial-collage static for the stomach angle"), NOT an original visual idea. **"Variations"** = headline/color/crop swaps within a concept.

Volume reality: mature accounts run 20–60 concepts/month → 60–300 ads/month. Concept-level win rate ~5–20%, commonly quoted as "1 in 10 becomes a scale winner." Superside: 20–30 concepts/month; MuteSix: "30–50 ad variations monthly." Statics still drive ~60–70% of Meta conversions (Social Media Examiner, Dec 2025).

## 2. Where concepts come from (q2, q5, q6)

Not from creative leaps. From **systematic recombination of researched inputs**:

- **Customer language is the raw material.** Review mining → verbatim phrases become hooks. The only S124 gauntlet ad the user even considered (#1, Ledger) carried real customer words — consistent.
- **Angle matrix**: segment × pain × proof type × offer, crossed with awareness stage (Schwartz's 5 stages are standard industry equipment — Motion, Outbrain teach them directly).
- **Competitor structure** (not surface): "Don't copy other brands — study their structure" (Leadenforce).
- **Frameworks over genius**: AdOven's "Big Book of Static Ad Frameworks" — high-performing statics "followed specific, repeatable patterns… like discovering the Periodic Table of Image Ads." Darkroom: performance creative is "a system that learns, not a single asset… built for volume and learning, not for one perfect asset."
- Sarah Levinger's hook framework (one of the few verbatim practitioner frameworks retrievable): 1) pattern-breaking distinction (Von Restorff), 2) future pacing (vivid desired state), 3) a sharp insight that explains why current results suck. Note: this is a HOOK procedure, not a visual-idea procedure.
- Motion's 550k-ad study: **"Offer-only hooks ranked near the top for hit rate… Text-only ads, product images with overlays, and GIFs all lead to more winners… clarity beats being clever."**

## 3. What actually wins as a static (q3) — the format ranking

Practitioner consensus 2025–26, for direct response:

| Rank | Format | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Top | **Lo-fi / "ugly" native statics** | "Unpolished, authentic-looking ads convert at 3–5× the rate of polished production" (Savannah Sanchez). "Pretty Ad Problem: looks like an obvious advertisement → ad blindness." |
| Top | **Comparison-led (us-vs-them)** | Sanchez 2026: one of three static types "outperforming overly polished videos"; "clear contrasts" = strong Andromeda signal. |
| High | **Notes-app / checklist / text-heavy reminder** | "Familiar formats (notes, checklists, comparisons)" + "save-worthy visuals" doing the "heavy lifting" in 2026. |
| Mid | **Testimonial / review screenshots** | Warm traffic + skeptical categories. |
| Mid | **Advertorial / listicle** | Education-heavy products, mid-funnel. |
| Unproven | **Memes** | No quantified consensus. |
| **Bottom** | **Polished designed "poster"** | Used for brand/launches/luxury; "no longer considered the top-performing DR format"; loses head-to-head to lo-fi in conversion tests. |

⚠️ **This collides with the S124 designed-poster target lock.** Caveats before over-rotating: the 3–5× figure is one practitioner's claim, not platform data; the user's three reference ads ARE designed posters and exist in the Indian market context; and the genericness problem the user hates may be about *sellability to founders* (demo bar) more than *convertibility* (spend bar). But the direction of the evidence is unambiguous: the conversion-optimal static is mostly UNDER-designed, not better-designed.

## 4. Andromeda economics (q4)

- "Creative testing replaces audience testing as the central lever." Targeting is dead as the lever.
- **Diversity beats volume**: near-duplicates collapse into one Entity ID — "Five product shots with slightly different copy variations? Meta sees that as one ad now" (Motion). 20 near-identical ads = one signal. Diversity dimensions: hook, frame/format, offer, audience.
- Structure: one campaign, one ad set, many genuinely-different creatives. Dozens per ad set is now fine.
- Fatigue: after ~4 exposures conversion probability drops ~45% → constant refresh; high-spend creatives fatigue every 2–4 weeks.
- This validates the S117 "Meta algorithmically penalizes sameness" thesis and the diverse-SET-per-angle direction.

## 5. India specifics (q7)

- Indian D2C Meta spend skews Reels-first; statics live mostly in retargeting/catalog. Hinglish dominates. Offer presentation: bundles/combos beat % off; MRP-vs-deal in one frame; urgency tied to time bands.
- Quick-commerce ad consensus: "fresh, trendy creative that doesn't feel like advertising" (Zepto audience); "clean, clear product shots... show price" (Blinkit audience); "static ads are pretty much dead in quick commerce — DPAs outperform statics 40–60%."
- **The leap-class work is brand-agency work**: TWT × Manja "behind the protein" (2025) is an agency brand campaign; Blinkit's riddle OOH is a brand/OOH campaign. These get shared and remembered — they are not the Meta DR statics those same brands run for conversion.
- Named in-market tooling: Hawky, Motion, Triple Whale for creative analytics at ₹5L+/mo spend.

## 6. AI-tools landscape (q8)

- Stack in use: AdCreative.ai, AdMake, Predis, Creatopy, Canva AI, Meta Advantage+ Creative (auto-variants, claimed +14% cost-per-result), Arc Ads, Sora-class image gen, Motion analytics.
- "AI slop" is now mainstream vocabulary (Merriam-Webster 2025 WOTY adjacent). Practitioner line: AI = variants/scale; human = **concept, strategy, taste, curation**. Cambridge study: co-creation only improves when humans *refine* (idea co-development), not when they generate more.
- Matches the S122 platform thesis (renderer = commodity, we sell the creative director). But note what the "human part" actually is in industry practice: **picking the angle and curating** — not inventing award-class visuals.

## 7. What this says about "where it's going wrong" (for discussion, not prescription)

1. **We've been grading against the wrong bar.** The S124 gauntlet was CD-graded ("above average craft/leap"). The industry's bar is angle-audience match at volume, judged by spend. The user's own Type-A lens memory said this; the research confirms it empirically. The leap belongs to a different product class (brand campaigns).
2. **The "average" outputs may be closer to market-correct than the verdicts implied** — IF the angle/offer/customer-language layer is right. The gauntlet held ONE angle constant and varied execution; the industry holds execution cheap and varies ANGLES.
3. **What our pipeline lacks vs the industry process isn't a leap generator.** It's: (a) verbatim customer language as hook raw material (we have research binders that mine VOC — are we using the words?), (b) a proven-DR-format repertoire (us-vs-them, review-roundup, notes-app, offer-led — formats are FUNCTIONAL structures tied to awareness stage, not a style menu), (c) concept diversity ACROSS angles per set, (d) offer/price/bundle presence in the ad at all.
4. **The differentiation tension is real and unresolved**: if the convert-optimal static is "ugly," what do we sell founders who can already make ugly in Canva? Possible reframe: the platform's edge = the *system* (research → angles → diverse concept sets → kill discipline → ledger), not the poster. The designed poster might be the demo-day wow, not the daily workhorse. **Real spend remains the never-consulted judge** — and this research makes the ₹20–30k beta more decisive, not less.
5. **Limit of this research**: named-practitioner depth (Dara Denney, Barry Hott, Nick Theriot et al.) is locked in video/socials — q5 returned honest "couldn't retrieve." If we want their actual processes, the next step is YouTube-transcript / newsletter deep-dives, not more search.

## Source-quality caveats

Heavy reliance on agency blogs and Instagram-reel practitioner content; almost no platform-published datasets exist. Specific numbers (3–5× lo-fi claim, +22% ROAS post-Andromeda, 40–60% DPA-vs-static) are practitioner-reported, unaudited. The harness's no-gap-filling prompt held: several queries explicitly flagged what could NOT be found.

# Round 2 (same day): are the classic formats still current? (`r2-q1..q6`)

Asked because the user challenged "proven formats" as possibly stale. Verdict: **the formats are current — verified with 2026 evidence — because format ≠ execution.**

## R2.1 Direct evidence the classics run at scale right now

- **Motion's live Kajabi breakdown (page live June 2026):** Kajabi runs 80 active Meta ads, ships ~17 new creatives/week, and **"Us Vs Them" is 13% of the active library** — real spend on the format today.
- **Stormy AI 2026 Advantage+ playbook** lists the core concept set to feed Meta as: UGC testimonial / product-focused / **"Educational/Listicle: 'Top 5 reasons why…' graphics"** / **"Comparison: 'Us vs. Them' charts."**
- **2026 practitioner tier list (April 2026 reel):** image formats S-tier = "1. Us vs Them, 2. Trending Memes, 3. Tweets Style Images."
- **ZenWeb (2026):** "Static ads handle direct-response with clear offers. Reels handle scale. UGC handles credibility." — statics = the DR workhorse role in a 3-format mix.

## R2.2 Fatigue is ad-level, never format-level (the load-bearing distinction)

- **Omneky Q1 2026:** creative fatigue degrades performance ~28% after 3–4 weeks of continuous serving — that's the *individual creative*, not the pattern.
- **r2-q6 (durability):** no source anywhere documents a proven DR format dying market-wide. Formats persist because they're containers for constant buyer psychology (social proof, contrast, loss aversion, PAS); what dies is the specific expression after exposure saturation. AdLibrary's method: find ads running 45/60/90 days in the category — still-running = still paying back.
- Implication: "old format" is not a defect. Freshness lives in the HOOK + angle + execution; the format is the durable chassis. (Motion: hook sits at the top of the creative hierarchy — refresh that first.)

## R2.3 One genuine kill: before/after in health categories

**Forge Digital (2026 supplements guide):** "Avoid dramatic before-and-after images for weight loss, muscle gain, or skin conditions" — **Meta policy/compliance risk, not fatigue**. Replacements: lifestyle-in-routine, clean product shots + benefit callouts, simple ingredient infographics. Directly relevant to TWT-class brands. Drop before/after from our health-category repertoire.

## R2.4 Statics' honest place in the 2026 mix

- Single-image advertiser usage fell ~8% 2024→2025; Reels = 53% of IG ad inventory Q4 2025; Meta's own data: carousels deliver 30–50% lower cost-per-conversion than single images. Video carries reach/scale.
- BUT statics still drive ~60–70% of conversions (SME cross-account analysis) and are universally kept as the conversion/BOF workhorse. A static-first product is defensible; the mix context should be known.

## R2.5 Velocity benchmark (product-relevant)

Motion's 550k-ad/$1.3B dataset: **top accounts test 15–25 new creatives/week vs 3–5 for average accounts**; 2026 top-account benchmark = 50+ active variants while most advertisers run 3–5. The velocity gap IS the product opportunity — founders can't produce this throughput; the system can.

## R2.6 Emerging formats: nothing has displaced the classics (in evidence)

Fake-UI statics (iMessage/Reddit/search-bar), AI-surreal "reality-bending," editorial-grade AI statics, founder-letter statics — all visible in the wild / pushed by tools, **none with verified Meta performance data**. The honest 2026 picture: classics confirmed, novelties unproven.

## R2.7 India gap

r2-q5 returned an honest "could not verify" — no indexed Indian Ad-Library format breakdowns. Per-category, per-market currency must come from **our comp binder** (revealed winners = long-running ads in the target category) — which is exactly what it was built to do. The format repertoire should be a PRIOR that comp evidence updates per brand, not a fixed menu.
