# Clean Premium

Dark, moody product presentations with colored aura glow, glass/translucent overlays, and floating composition. Premium aspirational aesthetic — the visual language of AG1, OURA, Ridge-style ads. Photorealistic mixed-media built on a 3-layer foundation: photo base, graphic design frame, typography + info.

**Prerequisites:** Hook-bank file must exist. Main Agent triggers hook-methodology skill first.

---

## Style Overview

**What it is:** Product floats in a dark void (near-black or deep brand color) with a colored aura glow radiating behind it. Glass or translucent overlay elements create foreground depth layers. Dramatic rim lighting defines the product edges while the aura backlight creates a halo effect. Typography is condensed sans-serif, minimal, refined. The dark negative space IS the design — the product is the only illuminated object in the frame.

**Why it works:**
- Dark backgrounds signal luxury and exclusivity — the product earns the viewer's attention, it doesn't beg for it
- Colored aura glow creates a signature brand moment (ownable color = ownable identity)
- Glass/translucent overlays add depth and sophistication without environmental complexity
- Floating product with no visible surface implies weightlessness and future-forward positioning
- Rim lighting sculpts the product into a hero object — dramatic, aspirational, desirable
- High contrast (dark background, lit product) stops the scroll in bright, cluttered feeds

**Visual Characteristics:**

| Element | Specification |
|---------|---------------|
| Background | Dark (near-black or deep brand color), moody atmosphere |
| Product | Floating with no visible surface, realistic sub-shadow beneath |
| Aura glow | Colored light emission behind product (brand primary or accent), soft edges, 40-60% opacity |
| Glass overlays | Translucent/frosted glass shapes creating depth layers in foreground |
| Lighting | Dramatic rim lighting + colored aura backlight |
| Typography | Condensed sans-serif (Gotham Condensed / Barlow Condensed style), minimal, refined |
| Negative space | Dark space IS the design — product floats in it |

## Step 5: Design Principles

### 1. Dark Canvas as Luxury Signal
- Background is near-black (#080808 to #0F0F0F range) or a very deep brand color at 5-10% brightness
- The darkness is not empty — it is the design. It signals exclusivity, premium positioning, and confidence
- No gradient in the background. Pure dark void with possible subtle radial darkening at edges (vignette)
- The dark canvas creates maximum contrast for the product and aura, making both impossible to ignore
- Dark space should occupy 50-60% of the total composition

### 2. Aura Glow as Brand Signature
- Colored light emission radiates from behind the product in the brand's primary or accent color
- Aura is soft-edged, never sharp or geometric — it bleeds organically into the dark canvas
- Opacity range: 40-60% at peak intensity, fading to transparent at edges
- The aura occupies 25-35% of the frame area (larger than the product, smaller than the dark space)
- One aura color per composition — do not mix multiple glow colors in a single ad

### 3. Floating Product (No Surface)
- Product has no visible surface, table, shelf, or ground beneath it
- A subtle sub-shadow (5-10% opacity, diffused) sits directly beneath the product to prevent it from feeling pasted on
- The sub-shadow is the same hue as the aura glow, not black — it connects the product to its light source
- Product should appear to levitate naturally, as if suspended by the aura energy
- Product occupies 25-35% of the frame — large enough to identify details, small enough for the dark canvas to breathe

### 4. Glass/Translucent Depth Layers
- 1-3 frosted or translucent glass shapes create foreground depth layers
- Glass shapes are semi-transparent (15-30% opacity) with subtle blur and edge refraction
- At least one glass element partially overlaps the product (creating a layering effect)
- Glass shapes use neutral tones (white, light gray) or very faint brand color tint
- Shapes can be geometric (rectangles, rounded rectangles) or organic (flowing curves) depending on brand character

### 5. Condensed Refined Typography
- Condensed sans-serif typeface (Gotham Condensed, Barlow Condensed, or similar)
- Headline in white or very light neutral against the dark canvas — maximum legibility
- ALL CAPS with generous letter-spacing (tracking +50 to +100) for refined, premium feel
- Text is minimal — headline and CTA only, no body copy or supporting text
- Font weight: Medium to SemiBold (not Extra Bold — refinement over impact)

### 6. Rim Lighting as Product Definition
- Dramatic edge lighting traces the product's silhouette against the dark background
- Rim light color: warm white, cool white, or a subtle tint of the aura color
- Rim lighting intensity is highest on the side opposite the aura glow (creating dimensional separation)
- The rim light makes the product's form legible against the dark canvas without flooding the scene with light
- Secondary fill light at 10-20% intensity prevents the product's front face from going completely dark

## Mobile Constraint Integration

Clean Premium layouts use the dark canvas to create natural visual hierarchy on mobile. Key rules per aspect ratio:

### 4:5 (1080x1350) — Feed Optimized
- Headline in top 15-25% of frame (below 14% safe zone on Stories, comfortable in feed)
- Product + aura centered, occupying the middle 35-50% band of the frame
- CTA at approximately 65-70% down (above bottom-35% safe zone)
- Glass overlays span from upper to mid frame, creating depth without crowding text zones
- Dark canvas dominates corners and edges — product is the illuminated center

### 1:1 (1080x1080) — Feed Standard / Carousel
- Headline in top 20-30% of frame
- Product + aura can be centered or offset to rule-of-thirds with headline in opposing space
- CTA at approximately 75-80% down
- Glass overlays frame the product from two sides, creating a centered stage effect
- Square format allows for slightly wider aura glow spread (horizontal room)

### 9:16 (1080x1920) — Reels / Stories
- Headline at 16-28% from top (below 14% status bar safe zone)
- Product + aura centered at 35-50% from top (upper-center of the vertical frame)
- CTA at approximately 55-60% down (well above 65% safe zone boundary)
- Glass overlays stretch vertically, matching the tall format
- Dark canvas above and below the product creates dramatic vertical negative space
- Strict single-column stacking — no side-by-side elements

| No glass overlays (just product on dark background) | Glass overlays required for depth — at least 1-2 translucent elements |
| Multiple aura glow colors in one composition | Single brand color aura — one glow, one identity |
| Bright or warm overall scene lighting | Dark canvas with dramatic rim lighting + colored aura only |
| Product sitting on a surface or pedestal | Product floating in void with no implied ground plane |
| Serif or decorative typography | Condensed sans-serif only — refined, minimal, premium |
| Body copy, feature lists, or supporting text | Headline + CTA only — the visual does the communicating |
| Sharp geometric edges on the aura glow | Soft organic glow emission, no hard boundaries |
| Clay, illustrated, or non-photorealistic product rendering | Photorealistic product with realistic materials, reflections, and lighting |
| Borders or frames of any kind | Edge-to-edge dark canvas, glass overlays define internal composition |
| Bright white or colorful backgrounds behind the product | Near-black void is mandatory — the darkness IS the premium signal |

## Quality Checklist

### Product
- [ ] Product occupies 25-35% of frame
- [ ] Product is photorealistic with realistic materials and surface detail
- [ ] Product floats with no visible surface or ground beneath it
- [ ] Sub-shadow is present in aura color at 5-10% opacity (not black)
- [ ] Product is identifiable as this specific brand's product (Logo Swap Test passes)

### Aura/Lighting
- [ ] Aura glow uses brand primary or accent color (one color only)
- [ ] Aura is soft-edged and organic (no geometric boundaries)
- [ ] Aura opacity is 40-60% at peak, fading to transparent at edges
- [ ] Rim lighting defines the product silhouette against the dark canvas
- [ ] Secondary fill light at 10-20% prevents product front face from going fully dark

### Glass
- [ ] At least 1-2 glass/translucent overlay elements present
- [ ] Glass overlays are 15-30% opacity with subtle blur and edge refraction
- [ ] At least one glass element partially overlaps the product
- [ ] Glass shapes create depth layering (foreground glass, mid-ground product, background aura)

### Typography
- [ ] Condensed sans-serif typeface (not serif, not decorative)
- [ ] ALL CAPS with generous letter-spacing (+50 to +100 tracking)
- [ ] White or very light neutral color against dark canvas
- [ ] Headline + CTA only (no body copy)
- [ ] Weight is Medium to SemiBold (refined, not heavy)

### Mobile
Follow shared mobile checklist in SKILL.md § Mobile Safe Zone Rules.
- [ ] Product + aura glow combined occupy at least 35% of frame

### Composition
- [ ] Dark canvas occupies 50-60% of total composition
- [ ] Background is near-black (5-10% brightness) with no gradient
- [ ] No scene elements, environments, or lifestyle objects
- [ ] No borders or frames
