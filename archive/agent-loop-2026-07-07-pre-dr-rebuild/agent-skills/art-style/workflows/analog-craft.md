# Analog Craft

Deliberate lo-fi warmth with film grain, torn paper collage layers, hand-cut elements, and imperfect serif typography. The "tactile rebellion" aesthetic that feels physical in a digital feed. Think: Jones Road Beauty, artisan DTC brands, craft food packaging that looks like it was assembled by hand.

**Prerequisites:** Hook-bank file must exist. Main Agent triggers hook-methodology skill first.

---

## Style Overview

**What it is:** A photorealistic mixed-media style built on a 3-layer foundation. The product is treated as a "found photograph" -- printed on paper with torn or rough edges -- layered onto a tactile collage of kraft paper, newsprint, and handmade paper elements. Film grain, warm tungsten color science, and soft light leak artifacts give the entire composition the quality of a rediscovered analog artifact. Typography uses curvy serif faces (Recoleta, Cooper, Bookmania style) rendered with slight imperfection, as if hand-stamped or letterpress-printed. The result feels like a physical object -- something you could pick up and feel -- in a feed full of clean digital perfection.

**Why it works:**
- Tactile texture breaks the scroll pattern -- in a feed of smooth gradients and clean vectors, paper grain demands attention
- Film grain and warm tones trigger nostalgia and trust -- the visual shorthand for "authentic, handmade, real"
- Torn paper edges create organic visual interest without requiring 3D rendering or complex illustration
- Imperfect typography signals craft and intentionality -- the opposite of corporate polish
- Light leaks and analog artifacts add visual complexity that feels accidental and therefore genuine
- The collage format supports layered storytelling -- multiple paper elements can each carry information
- Physical-feeling textures create subconscious associations with quality, weight, and permanence

**Visual Characteristics:**

| Element | Specification |
|---------|---------------|
| Film grain | 8-15% visible grain overlay, film stock quality (not digital noise) |
| Color tone | Warm tungsten shift, slightly desaturated, analog color science |
| Light leaks | Soft orange/amber light leak streaks (film exposure artifacts) |
| Torn paper | Kraft, newsprint, handmade paper layers with torn fibrous edges |
| Product treatment | Product as a "found photograph" -- printed on paper, torn edges, slight curl |
| Typography | Curvy serif (Recoleta, Cooper, Bookmania style), slightly imperfect, hand-stamped quality |
| Texture | Visible paper grain, fabric weave, natural material surfaces throughout |
| Craft elements | Hand-drawn marks, pressed botanicals, wax seals, handwritten notes, rubber stamps |

## Step 5: Design Principles

### 1. Film Grain as Authenticity Signal
- Apply 8-15% visible grain across the entire composition -- this is not subtle background noise but a defining texture
- The grain should have the quality of high-ISO film stock (Kodak Portra 800 or Fuji 400H pushed), not digital noise
- Grain density should be slightly higher in shadow areas and lighter in highlights, mimicking real film behavior
- The grain unifies all layers of the collage -- paper, photograph, typography -- into a cohesive analog artifact
- Never apply grain only to the product photo; it must cover the full frame including paper layers and text

### 2. Warm Tungsten Color Science
- Shift the entire color temperature toward warm tungsten (approximately 3200K feel, not daylight 5600K)
- Desaturate slightly -- colors are rich but not vivid, as if printed on warm-toned paper stock
- Blacks are never pure black; they shift toward warm dark brown (#3A2E25 range)
- Whites are never pure white; they shift toward warm cream (#F5EDE0 range)
- Brand colors are applied through this warm filter -- a blue brand will read as a warm teal, a green brand as warm olive
- Shadows carry amber warmth, highlights carry soft golden quality

### 3. Torn Paper Depth
- Build the composition in 3-5 visible paper layers, each with distinct torn edges
- Torn edges must show paper fiber and thickness -- not clean geometric cuts or digital masks
- Each paper layer should be a different material: kraft, newsprint, cotton rag, tissue, handmade fiber paper
- Layers overlap with visible shadows between them (soft 3-6px shadows, warm-toned, directional)
- At least one torn edge should reveal a contrasting paper color beneath, creating depth through material
- Paper layers should feel like they were assembled by hand -- slightly crooked, not perfectly aligned

### 4. Light Leaks as Happy Accidents
- Include 1-2 soft orange or amber light leak streaks that cross the composition diagonally
- Light leaks should appear to originate from a frame edge, as if the film was briefly exposed during loading
- Opacity at 15-30% -- visible but not overwhelming, a subtle warmth that draws the eye
- Light leaks add warm highlights to areas they cross, slightly washing out detail in those zones
- Position light leaks to guide the eye toward the product photograph or headline
- Never use cool-toned or neon light leaks -- strictly warm amber and soft orange

### 5. Imperfect Typography
- Use curvy serif faces in the style of Recoleta, Cooper Black, or Bookmania -- warm, rounded, friendly
- Typography should have a slightly imperfect quality: uneven ink density, minor registration shifts, faint texture within letterforms
- This suggests letterpress, rubber stamp, or woodblock printing -- each letter has character
- Headline text can be slightly rotated (1-3 degrees) as if hand-placed
- Supporting text can appear handwritten or in a typewriter face for contrast with the serif headline
- Text color should feel printed, not digital -- slightly uneven, warm dark brown or deep brand color through the tungsten filter

### 6. Product as Found Object
- The product is never rendered directly in the scene -- it appears as a PHOTOGRAPH of the product, printed on paper
- The product photograph has its own torn or rough edges, as if torn from a magazine or printed on photo paper
- Slight curl, corner fold, or tape mark on the product photograph reinforces the "found" quality
- The photograph may have a thin white border (like a film print) or torn edges exposing paper fiber
- Product photo is the highest-fidelity element in the collage -- photorealistic within its paper frame
- The product photograph should occupy 30-40% of the total frame, anchoring the composition

## Mobile Constraint Integration

Analog Craft collages are inherently textural and benefit from mobile's intimate viewing distance -- the paper grain and craft details reward close viewing. Key rules per aspect ratio:

### 4:5 (1080x1350) -- Feed Optimized
- Product photograph positioned at center or center-right, anchoring the vertical composition
- Headline on the visible base paper layer above the main collage area (upper 20-30%)
- Paper layers arranged with primary collage elements in the central 50%
- CTA on a torn paper strip or stamped onto base layer at 68-72% down
- Craft elements distributed around the product photograph, not clustered
- Light leaks should cross the upper third, guiding eye from headline to product

### 1:1 (1080x1080) -- Feed Standard / Carousel
- Wider collage composition -- paper layers can extend horizontally
- Product photograph at center-left or center-right at rule-of-thirds
- Recipe cards, notes, or secondary paper elements can occupy the opposing side
- Headline in upper area on the base paper or largest paper layer
- CTA at 75-80% down, on a craft element or torn paper strip
- Square format benefits from asymmetric paper layer arrangement

### 9:16 (1080x1920) -- Reels / Stories
- Vertical paper stack -- layers arranged top to bottom, not side by side
- Headline in upper zone (below 14% safe zone) stamped onto base layer
- Product photograph at vertical center (40-55% down)
- Craft elements and supporting paper layers above and below the product
- CTA at 55-62% down, well above the 65% safe zone boundary
- The tall format amplifies the "stacked papers on a desk" quality
- Extra vertical space allows more paper layers and craft details

## Anti-Patterns

| Never | Instead |
|-------|---------|
| Clean geometric edges on paper elements | Torn fibrous edges with visible paper fiber and thickness |
| Selective saturation (product vivid, background muted) | Unified warm tungsten color grade across ALL elements |
| Polished serif typography (Playfair, Garamond) | Imperfect curvy serif (Recoleta, Cooper, Bookmania) with stamp/letterpress quality |
| Linen or canvas texture as primary background | Kraft paper, newsprint, handmade paper, or corrugated cardboard |
| Clean digital gradients | Analog light leaks, paper color variation, and film grain instead |
| Smooth polished surfaces | Visible paper grain, fiber texture, and material imperfection |
| Product rendered directly in scene | Product as a PHOTOGRAPH (printed, torn, polaroid, film print) |
| Geometric overlay shapes (circles, rectangles, arcs) | Torn paper layers, organic collage shapes, hand-placed elements |
| Cold, clinical, or blue-white lighting | Warm tungsten color science with amber shadows and golden highlights |
| Perfect symmetry or mathematical alignment | Hand-assembled arrangement with slight rotation and organic placement |
| Stock photo product presentation | Found-object treatment with wear, curl, tape, or coffee stain marks |
| Pure black or pure white anywhere | Warm dark brown (#3A2E25 range) and warm cream (#F5EDE0 range) |
| Digital noise or uniform grain | Film stock grain (denser in shadows, lighter in highlights) |
| Neon, cool, or digitally colored light leaks | Warm amber and soft orange light leaks only |

## Quality Checklist

### Film/Texture
- [ ] Film grain at 8-15%, film stock quality (not digital noise)
- [ ] Grain denser in shadows, lighter in highlights
- [ ] Warm tungsten color grade across the entire composition
- [ ] No pure blacks or pure whites -- all colors shifted warm
- [ ] Light leaks present (1-2), warm amber or orange, 15-30% opacity

### Paper Layers
- [ ] 3-5 distinct paper layers visible in the composition
- [ ] Each layer is a different paper type (kraft, newsprint, cotton, tissue, etc.)
- [ ] Torn edges show paper fiber and thickness on at least 2 layers
- [ ] Layers overlap with warm directional shadows between them (3-6px)
- [ ] Arrangement is slightly crooked, hand-assembled (not grid-aligned)

### Product
- [ ] Product appears as a PHOTOGRAPH (not rendered directly in scene)
- [ ] Photo treatment is specified (film print, polaroid, magazine tear, etc.)
- [ ] Photo has tactile qualities (torn edges, corner curl, tape, white border)
- [ ] Product within the photo is photorealistic and sharp
- [ ] Product photograph occupies 30-40% of total frame

### Craft Elements
- [ ] 2-3 hand-made craft elements present (botanical, stamp, handwriting, seal, etc.)
- [ ] Craft elements are brand-specific, not generic decorative marks
- [ ] All craft elements have analog quality (imperfect, warm-toned)
- [ ] Craft elements are distributed around the composition, not clustered

### Typography
- [ ] Curvy serif face used for headline (Recoleta, Cooper, Bookmania style)
- [ ] Typography has imperfect quality (uneven ink, slight rotation, stamp texture)
- [ ] Text color is warm (dark brown or deep brand color, never pure black)
- [ ] Supporting text uses contrasting format (handwriting, typewriter, small serif)
- [ ] Text positioned on paper layers, not floating on the composition

### Mobile
Follow shared mobile checklist in SKILL.md -- Mobile Safe Zone Rules.
- [ ] Product photograph and headline legible at 300px width

### Composition
- [ ] Overall collage feels hand-assembled and tactile
- [ ] No digital perfection -- organic, warm, slightly imperfect throughout
- [ ] Brand specificity: collage elements reference brand's specific story
- [ ] Logo Swap Test passes -- material choices are ownable by this brand
