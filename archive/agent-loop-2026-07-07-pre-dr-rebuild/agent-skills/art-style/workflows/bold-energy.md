# Bold Energy

Saturated color blocks, dynamic product placement, oversized type, and high-energy visual impact. Photorealistic mixed-media built on a 3-layer foundation: photo-base product cutout, graphic design frame of 2-3 color blocks, and oversized typography that IS the design element. Designed to stop the scroll through sheer visual force.

**Prerequisites:** Hook-bank file must exist. Main Agent triggers hook-methodology skill first.

---

## Style Overview

**What it is:** A photorealistic mixed-media style where saturated color blocks divide the frame into 2-3 bold fields, a photorealistic product cutout sits at their intersection tilted at a dynamic angle, and oversized hero typography occupies 40-50% of the frame. Geometric energy accents (circles, arrows, lightning bolts, triangles) add kinetic punch. The overall feel is loud, unapologetic, and engineered for thumb-stop impact. Think Obvi supplement ads, Liquid Death can placements, and high-energy D2C brands that lead with visual force.

**Why it works:**
- Saturated color blocks create instant visual contrast that breaks the scroll pattern
- Dynamic angles (10-15 degree tilt) inject kinetic energy into a static image
- Oversized type at 40-50% of frame delivers the hook before the viewer consciously decides to read
- Product at the color block intersection draws the eye to the natural focal point
- Geometric accents (arrows, lightning bolts) create directional energy and movement
- The photorealistic product cutout grounds the design in a real, purchasable object
- Asymmetric, high-tension layouts feel alive and urgent compared to centered, balanced ads
- Zero uncanny valley risk — real product photo combined with flat graphic design

**Visual Characteristics:**

| Element | Specification |
|---------|---------------|
| Color blocks | 2-3 saturated color fields dividing the frame (diagonal, angular, or vertical splits) |
| Product | Photorealistic cutout placed at intersection of color blocks, 10-15 degree tilt |
| Dynamic angle | Entire composition tilted or product at dynamic angle for kinetic energy |
| Geometric shapes | Bold circles, triangles, arrows, lightning bolts as energy accents |
| Typography | Oversized hero type at 40-50% of frame — text IS a design element |
| Layout | Product at color block intersection, asymmetric, high tension |

## Step 5: Design Principles

### 1. Color Block Architecture
- Frame divided into 2-3 saturated color fields — these blocks ARE the composition structure
- Color blocks derive from the brand palette: primary becomes the dominant field, secondary the supporting field, accent for geometric details
- Division lines are diagonal, angular, or vertical — never perfectly horizontal (horizontal is static, angles are dynamic)
- Color fields are fully saturated, flat, and bold — no gradients, no textures, no subtle transitions
- The intersection of color blocks is the focal point — this is where the product lives

### 2. Product at the Intersection
- The photorealistic product cutout sits exactly where color blocks meet — the natural focal point
- Product is a real photograph with clean cutout edges (not illustrated, not clay, not rendered)
- Product occupies 25-35% of the frame — large enough to be the anchor but not so large it crowds the type
- The product interacts with both color fields — it belongs to neither block alone, bridging both
- Subtle shadow beneath the product (4-6px, directional) grounds it in the composition without 3D depth

### 3. Dynamic Angle
- Product tilted 10-15 degrees from vertical — never straight, never beyond 20 degrees (which reads as falling)
- The tilt direction should complement the color block division angle — creating visual harmony in the energy
- The tilt creates kinetic energy: the product looks like it just arrived or is about to launch
- Optional: the entire composition can be tilted 3-5 degrees for additional dynamism (subtle, not disorienting)
- The dynamic angle is what separates Bold Energy from static product-on-gradient shots

### 4. Oversized Type as Structure
- Headline text occupies 40-50% of the frame — it is not a caption, it is a structural design element
- Text interacts with the color blocks: headline can span both blocks, sit on one block, or break across the division
- Sans-serif, Extra Bold or Black weight — Impact, Bebas Neue, Anton, or Montserrat Black
- ALL CAPS for the headline — this is a shout, not a whisper
- One key word in the headline can use the accent color or a contrasting block color for emphasis
- Type can overlap the product slightly (10-15%) — the layers interact

### 5. Geometric Energy Accents
- Bold geometric shapes add kinetic energy and directional force to the composition
- Shapes include: circles, triangles, arrows, lightning bolts, starbursts, speed lines, chevrons
- Accents use the brand accent color or white at 60-90% opacity
- Place accents to create directional flow: guide the eye from headline to product to CTA
- 3-5 geometric accents per composition — enough for energy, not so many it becomes cluttered
- Accents can overlap color block boundaries, reinforcing the energetic, boundary-breaking feel

### 6. Maximum Saturation
- Every color in the composition is at or near maximum saturation — this is not a muted, subtle style
- Color blocks are pure, vivid brand colors — no washed-out tints, no pastel interpretations
- The contrast between adjacent color blocks should be high (complementary or split-complementary relationships)
- White or near-black elements provide relief and prevent the saturated fields from competing
- The saturation is the scroll-stopper: Bold Energy ads should be visible from across the room

## Mobile Constraint Integration

Bold Energy layouts use saturated color blocks and oversized type that remain impactful at every screen size. Key rules per aspect ratio:

### 4:5 (1080x1350) — Feed Optimized
- Diagonal color block split running upper-left to lower-right
- Headline stacked on the dominant block in the upper 50%
- Product at the block intersection, centered or slightly right of center
- CTA on the secondary block at 70-72% down
- Geometric accents flanking the product, 3-4 accents maximum
- The diagonal split and headline should be legible at thumbnail in the feed

### 1:1 (1080x1080) — Feed Standard / Carousel
- Diagonal or angular split works well in the square format
- Headline can split across both blocks (problem on one, solution on the other)
- Product centered at the intersection
- CTA at 75-78% down on the secondary block
- Most flexible format — vertical split also works for side-by-side contrast
- The square format amplifies the confrontational energy of the split

### 9:16 (1080x1920) — Reels / Stories
- Vertical or steep diagonal split (top block / bottom block)
- Headline on the upper block, below 14% safe zone, stacked at 40% of frame
- Product at the block intersection, at approximately 40-50% vertical position
- CTA on the lower block at 58-62% down, well above 65% safe zone
- Single-column stacking: headline, product, CTA — all vertically aligned
- Geometric accents flanking the product horizontally within the narrow format
- The tall format amplifies the vertical energy of stacked bold type

---

## Anti-Patterns

| Never | Instead |
|-------|---------|
| Muted, pastel, or desaturated color tones | Fully saturated color blocks at maximum intensity |
| Clay textures, 3D rendered, or illustrated product | Photorealistic product cutout — real photograph |
| Subtle, quiet, or restrained compositions | Loud, high-energy, unapologetic visual force |
| Centered symmetric layouts | Asymmetric composition with diagonal or angular tension |
| Small text as caption or overlay | Oversized type at 40-50% of frame as structural element |
| Gradients within color blocks | Flat, solid, fully saturated color fields |
| Product straight-on without dynamic angle | Product tilted 10-15 degrees for kinetic energy |
| More than 3 color blocks | 2-3 blocks maximum for clear architecture |
| Lifestyle scenes or environmental backgrounds | Abstract color block architecture with cutout product |
| Serif, script, or decorative fonts | Bold geometric sans-serif (Impact, Bebas Neue, Anton) |
| Borders or frames containing the composition | Energy breaks boundaries — elements can extend to edges |
| Scattered, random geometric shapes | Intentional geometric accents creating directional energy flow |

---

## Quality Checklist

### Color Blocks
- [ ] 2-3 saturated color blocks dividing the frame
- [ ] Division is diagonal, angular, or vertical (not horizontal)
- [ ] Colors are fully saturated — no pastels, no muted tones
- [ ] Block colors derive from brand palette
- [ ] High contrast between adjacent blocks

### Product
- [ ] Photorealistic product cutout (not clay, not illustrated, not rendered)
- [ ] Positioned at the intersection of color blocks
- [ ] Tilted 10-15 degrees for dynamic angle
- [ ] Occupies 25-35% of frame
- [ ] Subtle directional shadow grounding it in the composition
- [ ] Product is recognizable as this specific brand (Logo Swap Test)

### Typography
- [ ] Headline occupies 40-50% of frame — text is a structural element
- [ ] ALL CAPS, Extra Bold / Black weight, geometric sans-serif
- [ ] Key word has distinctive accent color treatment
- [ ] Text interacts with color blocks (spanning, sitting on, breaking across)
- [ ] Headline overlaps product by 10-15% (layers interact)
- [ ] Legible at 300px thumbnail width

### Geometry
- [ ] 3-5 geometric energy accents (circles, arrows, lightning, triangles)
- [ ] Accents create directional energy flow (headline to product to CTA)
- [ ] Accent color is brand accent or white at 60-90% opacity
- [ ] Accents are intentional, not decorative scatter

### Mobile
Follow shared mobile checklist in SKILL.md § Mobile Safe Zone Rules.
- [ ] Color block division visible at thumbnail scale
- [ ] Product and key headline word legible at 300px width

### Composition
- [ ] Layout is asymmetric with high visual tension
- [ ] Energy flows directionally through the composition
- [ ] Color blocks, product, type, and geometry form a unified kinetic system
- [ ] The ad is LOUD — visible from across the room at full saturation
