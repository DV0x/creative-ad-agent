## Hook 1
**Type:** Contrast
**Source:** Value Props ("100% RBI repo rate - Double the typical 50% rate") + Pain Points ("Interest with asterisks")
**Target:** Young Urban Indians (25-40)
**Hook:** "Your bank gives you half. We give you 100%."
**Body:** While traditional banks keep 50% of the RBI repo rate for themselves, slice passes on the full 100%. No asterisks. No fine print. Just more money in your account—compounding daily.
**CTA:** Get what's yours
**Psychology:** Contrast hook creates immediate tension (half vs. full). Triggers fairness instinct—"I'm being cheated." The word "yours" reinforces ownership and control.
