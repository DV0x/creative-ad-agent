## Hook 1
**Type:** Direct Address
**Source:** ICP Pain Points ("I start strong but lose motivation after a few weeks") + Brand Voice (challenge-based)
**Target:** Primary - Active Lifestyle Enthusiasts
**Hook:** "Your excuses are getting old."
**Body:** New gear. New mindset. The only thing between you and your next PR is the decision to show up.
**CTA:** Start moving
**Psychology:** Pattern interrupt through confrontation. Uses their own internal dialogue against them. Creates productive discomfort that motivates action rather than deflates.
