## Hook 1
**Type:** Contrast
**Source:** Pain Points ("Premium skincare brands charge 3-5x for the same active ingredients") + Value Props (₹284-₹599 pricing)
**Target:** Educated beauty consumers / Middle-income professionals
**Hook:** "Same actives. 3x less."
**Body:** Clinical-grade Vitamin C, Salicylic Acid, and Retinal—the same ingredients in premium brands, at prices that make sense.
**CTA:** See the percentages yourself
**Psychology:** Pattern interrupt through direct comparison. Creates cognitive dissonance—"wait, same quality for less?"—that demands investigation.
