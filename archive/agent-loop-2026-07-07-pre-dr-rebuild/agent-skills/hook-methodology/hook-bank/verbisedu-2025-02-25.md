## Hook 1

**Type:** Direct Address + Problem-Solution
**Source:** Customer Language & VoC Signals ("Confused about choosing the right course or university") + Pain Points (University selection confusion)
**Target:** First-Time International Applicants
**Funnel Stage:** TOF (Awareness)
**Psychological Driver:** Curiosity + Relatability
**Format Intention:** STATIC
**Hook:** "Confused About Which Exam to Take? We'll Tell You in 5 Minutes."
**Body:** Most students waste weeks choosing between IELTS, PTE, OET, or TOEFL. Verbis Edu matches you to the right exam based on your destination country, timeline, and strengths. Named trainers guide the entire journey from exam selection to university admission.
**CTA:** "Find Your Right Exam"
**Visual Direction:** Split-screen showing "Confused Student" (overwhelmed, looking at multiple exams) → "Clarity Moment" (student smiling with Partha/Vinutha shown beside them, exam clearly labeled)
**Logo-Swap Test:** PASS. "We'll tell you in 5 minutes" is specific to Verbis Edu's counseling-first positioning (competitors lead with exam features, not clarity framework)
**Psychology:** Pattern interrupt (unexpected speed offer) + self-reference ("Confused? That's you") + open-loop curiosity ("what will they tell me?")

**Visual Direction:** Countdown timer (March 31 date shown prominently) with calendar pages flipping toward deadline, university logos (Imperial, Sydney, Toronto) fading in background—student typing exam on screen shows urgency pacing

**Psychology:** Loss aversion (gap year = year lost) + scarcity (spots limited, deadline closes) + specificity (exact date) + mechanism clarity (when scores arrive)
