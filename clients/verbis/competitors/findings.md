### The paid-webinar creative playbook (what actually converts registrations)
1. **The expert's face is the product.** Big real cut-out. (For a trust purchase, people register for a *person*.)
2. **Price shown proudly as a no-brainer button** ("→ ₹99"), not hidden.
3. **"LIVE" + duration + format badge** ("3 Hours Live Masterclass").
4. **Event-detail block** — date / time with icons (the registration convention).
5. Problem/identity headline, huge, one word blown up + brand colour, often Hinglish.
6. Benefit bullets in outlined boxes.
7. Trust stamp / authority numbers / co-brand.
8. High-contrast **REGISTER NOW**.

> Note on price: Rahul Malodia proves the paid-live-class model scales — but at **₹99**. Verbis at **₹589** is 6× that for cold traffic. The model is valid; the creative has to justify the price harder (value stack + ₹3,000 exam coupon). Worth flagging a cheaper/entry-price test to the client.

## How this shapes our creative (borrow the skeleton, break the message)
The winning paid-webinar look is a **structured event poster** (face + price + date + live badge + bullets) — and every competitor's poster looks the same. So:
- **Borrow the proven skeleton** (trainer face + ₹589 button + "4-Hour Live Masterclass" badge + Sat 11 July block) so we read as a legit registrable event, not a random post.
- **Break on the message** — lead with our white-space hook (format-not-English → 79) and our unique proof (real 90/90 report), NOT another generic identity headline or AI campus.
- Result → two tracks: **A = event-poster workhorses** (registration conversion) and **B = native scroll-stoppers** (cheap top-of-funnel clicks). See `../concepts/concepts-v1.md`.
