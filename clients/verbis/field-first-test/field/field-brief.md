## Craft commons across endorsed ads
- Exactly 3 type sizes; one photo region OR pure typography — never both fighting.
- Chips/badges do the trust work: Google card, Pearson pill, logo strips, medals.
- Checkmark lists everywhere (de-risk); CTA always a filled rounded band.
- Two registers split the field: premium-clean (cream, 2 accents — Yocket/Leap/Verbis) vs
  mass-loud (saturated fields, 4+ colors — Gradding). Verbis's equity is premium-clean.
- Typos run for 83-101 days: the field is NOT punishing imperfection; endorsement ≠ polish.

## Copy layer (from the 144-ad dump)
- Hooks are QUESTIONS aimed at doubt ("Can you…?", "Think X is out of reach?", "Are you actually
  ready?") or named-object promises ("Master's in Germany", "Study in UK").
- Every rival CTA is FREE-something (consultation, report, shortlist, eligibility). NOBODY asks for a
  paid purchase directly except Verbis itself.
- Urgency is fake everywhere ("filling fast", "act now") — a REAL dated event (Sat 11 July) is scarce.
