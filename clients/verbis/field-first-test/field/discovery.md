### 3) Common creative patterns these brands are known for (what PMs actually talk about)

Across Indian ed‑tech/coaching brands that performance marketers praise or dissect, the patterns you’ll hear referenced repeatedly are:

- **Teacher/coach/founder as UGC talent**  
  - Talking‑head vertical videos with strong hooks in the first 2 seconds.  
  - Often shot on phones, then heavily text‑overlaid to match Meta’s mobile‑first best practices (big captions, quick pattern interrupts).[1][3]

- **Outcome- and proof‑centric creative**  
  - Salary jumps, rank achievements, number of students, success rates.  
  - Screenshots (offers, dashboards, test results) as *primary* visual, not supporting detail.  
  - Performance marketers refer to these as “proof‑wall” or “proof‑first” ad styles.

- **Offer‑driven DR layouts**  
  - Very clear, single‑minded offer: free class, scholarship test, limited‑time discounted batch.  
  - Big numerical anchors (₹, % off, number of tests/batches) and a simple CTA button area, aligning with generic Meta best practices around bold visuals and one core offer.[1][3]

- **Topical + calendar‑driven ad iterations**  
  - Creatives timed around exam notifications, result days, appraisals season, etc.  
  - Performance marketers often highlight Testbook/Adda247/Unacademy/Test‑prep ed‑techs as templates for this.

- **Regionalization and persona‑splits**  
  - Same template, swapped: language, exam name, city, student persona (dropper, working professional, college student).  
  - Inside PM communities, these brands are cited when arguing for “creative = targeting” in the Andromeda/creative‑first era of Meta.[3][4]
