## Creative 1 — The Sticky Notes  (construction: objection-wall, FITTR 50d)

Direct-response ad image for the Meta feed, 4:5.

LAYOUT: a real cream-painted wall fills the frame; across the top third, five pastel sticky notes
(mint, orange, blue, yellow, pink) pinned with small brass pins in a slightly uneven row. Center-left
below them, a large question headline; under it one short subhead line. Bottom-left, a filled CTA
band. Lower-right corner: the edge of a wooden desk with a spiral notebook and a wired headset —
the PTE test-day kit — softly out of focus. Generous margins; the wall's empty space is part of the
composition.

TYPE: the five notes are handwritten in blue ballpoint, natural uneven handwriting, one short phrase
each, exactly: "Speaking 58" · "Retake #3" · "Good English, low score" · "Templates don't work" ·
"Exam in 2 weeks". Headline in a heavy geometric sans, 800 weight, sentence case, deep navy, exactly:
"Are these keeping you under 79?" Subhead in the same family at regular weight, half the headline
size, exactly: "One Saturday. The format, decoded." Above the headline a small gold-outlined
rounded chip, uppercase, exactly: "LIVE · SAT 11 JULY · 4 HOURS". Below the subhead one small plain
line, exactly: "4.9★ · 491 Google reviews · Pearson-certified trainer". CTA band: rounded-rectangle,
solid warm yellow, navy bold text, exactly: "Claim your seat → ₹589".

LIGHT & FINISH: natural window light from the left, soft real shadows under each note, faint paper
curl at note corners; photographed-real texture on wall and desk, crisp designed type floating clean
over it. Surfaces are matte; the only saturated element is the yellow CTA.

PALETTE: warm cream wall, pastel notes, deep navy type, one yellow accent.

RENDER EXACTLY the seven quoted text strings above and no other text, no logos except a small
"VERBIS EDU" wordmark top-left, no people, no watermarks.

## Creative 3 — The Apology Letter  (construction: reverse-brag letter, Yocket 69d)

Direct-response ad image for the Meta feed, 4:5. This image is a typeset letter — typography is the
entire creative.

LAYOUT: a single centered column of text on off-white paper with a faint paper grain, generous even
margins on all sides. At the very top, a thin navy banner strip. The letter flows: opening line,
a three-item checklist block with slight left indent, two short paragraphs, a sign-off, an italic
P.S., and at the bottom a full-width rounded CTA band. Clear breathing space between every block —
the letter should feel unhurried.

TYPE: banner strip text in cream uppercase letterspaced small caps, exactly: "AN APOLOGY, FROM A PTE
TRAINER". Letter set in a serif with real italics, navy ink on paper. Opening line italic, exactly:
"Dear PTE aspirants, the industry owes you an apology." Then regular, exactly: "You were told to
improve your English. But you already:" followed by three checkbox lines, exactly: "✓ speak fluent
English" / "✓ work in English every day" / "✓ still score 55–65 in speaking". Paragraph, exactly:
"Your English was never the problem. PTE is scored by a machine, and the machine rewards a format.
Last batch, one student walked out with a 90/90 — the Pearson report is on our page." with "never",
"machine" and "90/90" in bold. Sign-off, exactly: "This Saturday, I'll show you the format. All 22
task types. Four hours. — Parthasarathy Bhaskara, Pearson-certified trainer". Italic P.S., exactly:
"P.S. If you've booked a retake already — come before you sit it. The seat is ₹589; a retake is
₹18,000." CTA band: solid warm yellow, navy bold sans, exactly: "Claim your seat → ₹589 · Sat 11 July".

FINISH: flat scanned-letter look, extremely subtle paper texture, no photography, no illustrations,
no decorative flourishes; the whitespace and the serif do the design work.

PALETTE: off-white paper, navy ink, one yellow CTA band.

RENDER EXACTLY the quoted text strings and no other text, no logos beyond a small navy "VERBIS EDU"
wordmark under the banner, no people, no watermarks.
