## Standing rules (from Verbis, hard-won — do not relitigate)
- **Brand marks go IN as reference images** to the render (edit / image-to-image), never stamped on afterwards and
  never drawn from a text description.
- **Real proof out-renders generated art** for a trust purchase — and a mortgage is the highest-trust purchase we've
  worked on yet. Harvest the real thing before rendering anything.
- **Never fabricate** a deal, a testimonial, a rate, or a timeline.
