The single clearest finding

$781 — 42% of the budget — went to engagement campaigns that produced zero applications. They bought 14,053 post reactions at 13¢ each. Cheap likes, no business. Across many ads, over three months, that is not a sample-size problem — it is a settled result.

Moved into the campaigns that already convert, that same $781 is worth roughly 70–110 more leads at the CPLs those campaigns are already achieving.

Two things to read carefully before acting

1. Eight conversions is a small sample. The “$21 per application” ad did that on a single conversion from $21 of spend — one data point, not a proven cost. Don’t bet a budget on any individual row above. The zero from $781 of engagement spend, however, is solid — that one you can act on today.

2. The number that decides everything is missing. Of the 106 leads, how many became real conversations? How many funded? And what is a funded deal worth in commission? If a funded construction deal is worth thousands, then applications at $21–$82 mean this account is badly under-spent. If those leads are junk, the cheap CPLs are a mirage. That answer has to come from the client.
