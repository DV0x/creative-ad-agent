# Forward pass — Step 2: Angle → format match + 6-ad set spec

**Process under test (S125 lock):** assembly, not invention. Each ad = strategist angle + verbatim customer language + proven DR format (chosen by awareness stage) + DR furniture. Formats from the S125 research repertoire (review-stack, contrast/checklist, label-hero, pull-quote flip, offer card, type poster). One designed-poster slot as the loud one. No leap required anywhere.

**Set-level requirements:** six visually distinct thumbnails; hooks all different; every ad carries the real pack (reference-bound); every ad has CTA furniture; zero unverifiable claims.

## FP-1 — Review-card stack ("No bloating at all.")
**Maker:** in-house performance designer, clean card layout (Figma energy).
**Surface:** warm cream field.
**Layout:** small burgundy-plum kicker top-left: `WHAT BUYERS KEEP SAYING`. Below it, three white rounded review cards with soft shadows, stacked left column, charcoal text, each with a small plum source chip:
- Card 1 (largest, bold): `"No bloating at all."` — chip `Amazon review`
- Card 2: `"I can finally have protein daily without stomach issues."` — chip `thewholetruthfoods.com review`
- Card 3: `"Easy on the stomach."` — chip `Amazon review`
**Pack:** right third, hero-large, standing on the cream surface with a true soft shadow, front face square.
**Furniture:** bottom full-width dark-plum bar, cream text: `Shop now → thewholetruthfoods.com`; small white chip near pack: `24g protein per scoop`.
**No star graphics** (values unverified).

## FP-2 — Checklist contrast ("NOT in this tub:")
**Maker:** in-house designer, flat print-poster layout.
**Surface:** dark plum field.
**Layout:** giant cream headline top: `NOT in this tub:` Below, a cream list in large type, each item struck through with a clean magenta strikethrough line:
- `Sucralose`
- `Gums`
- `Thickeners`
Beneath the list, small cream line: `Whey protein isolate + concentrate — declared on the front of the pack.`
Footer-left small cream quote: `"Easy on the stomach." — Amazon review`
**Pack:** bottom-right corner, medium, lit, exact reference.
**Furniture:** cream chip bottom-right under pack: `thewholetruthfoods.com`
**Fact basis:** pack "no artificial sweeteners or thickeners" + review "without artificial sweeteners or gums" + IG "don't add sucralose". No competitor column — contrast carried by strikethrough.

## FP-3 — Label-as-hero ("Read the label.")
**Maker:** product photographer + designer overlay.
**Surface:** full-bleed macro of the pack front, bright commercial light, slight 10° angle, every printed line legible and exactly as reference.
**Layout:** top cream band, charcoal headline: `Read the label. That's the whole ad.` Two white callout chips with thin charcoal pointer lines:
- chip → small gold dot/Trustified line: `Trustified — third-party verified`
- chip → plum badge: `24g protein per scoop`
**Furniture:** bottom-left charcoal-on-cream chip: `#nothingtohide`; bottom-right plum chip, cream text: `Shop now · thewholetruthfoods.com`

## FP-4 — Complaint flip ("More like cocoa and milk…")
**Maker:** editorial type designer, pull-quote poster.
**Surface:** dark plum field.
**Layout:** small gold kicker top: `AN ACTUAL COMPLAINT:` Giant cream serif quote, three lines: `"More like cocoa and milk, not like a milkshake."` Attribution small cream: `— Amazon review`. Then bold gold line, larger: `Exactly.` Small cream sub: `No artificial sweeteners. Tastes like real cocoa.`
**Pack:** bottom-LEFT, medium, true shadow (critic fix — diverge from FP-2's silhouette).
**Furniture:** cream chip, plum text, bottom-RIGHT: `Try it → thewholetruthfoods.com`
**Mandate served:** taste framed honestly upfront (strategy requires); complaint-as-pitch is #nothingtohide in action.
**Critic verdict:** set passed with 3 copy fixes + this divergence; no kills. Full verdict in critic exchange (carry-flags: no causal connectors in FP-2; check rendered pack weight vs "1KG" at vision gate).

## FP-5 — Offer card (BOF)
**Maker:** performance designer, clean e-commerce card.
**Surface:** warm cream field, pack dead-center, large, bright soft light, true shadow.
**Layout:** charcoal headline top, two lines: `Cleanest, lightest` / `whey protein. Ever.` (the pack's own claim). Under pack, three white spec chips in a row, charcoal text: `24g protein per scoop` · `Whey protein isolate + concentrate` · `Trustified-certified`
**Furniture:** full-width dark-plum CTA bar bottom, cream text: `Shop now — thewholetruthfoods.com`
**No price** (unconfirmed — honest flag).

## FP-6 — Type poster (the loud slot)
**Maker:** poster typographer; type IS the architecture.
**Surface:** warm cream field.
**Layout:** massive condensed charcoal type stacked left-aligned filling the upper two-thirds: `JUST` / `REORDERED` / `THE 1KG` / `PACK.` — a thick gold underline accent under `REORDERED`. Small plum chip below type, cream text: `— Instagram comment` (handle not in room — critic fix)
**Pack:** bottom-right, large, slightly overlapping the lowest type line (poster depth), true shadow.
**Furniture:** bottom-left charcoal small line: `Your first tub → thewholetruthfoods.com`
