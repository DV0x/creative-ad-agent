# Competitor Research — NYC Cosmetic Dermatology / Medspa

The brand under research is Ever/Body (see `research.md`). Its market is NYC
non-surgical aesthetic treatment — injectables, facials, lasers, body contouring.

## SkinSpirit

- Founded 2003 by Lynn Heublein and Dr. M Dean Vistnes, a Stanford-trained
  plastic surgeon. Over 20 years in the category.
- Widely described as **the largest provider of Botox and dermal filler in the
  United States.**
- Present in NYC (e.g. inside Nordstrom NYC, and a Tribeca clinic).
- Positioning: a relationship-based "maintenance" injector — trusted, expert,
  long-term care; the place "if you already look the way you want, holding it."
  Its authority rests on longevity and a surgeon-founder pedigree.

## Skinney Medspa

- New York City and Miami. A long-running, well-known NYC medspa with a heavy
  review base and visible ad presence.
- Positioning: "the premiere Botox provider in NYC," and the top CoolSculpting /
  EmSculpt provider in NYC; a "one-stop destination," promising "natural
  results" via "micro-droplet precision."
- Aggressive on offers: runs "10 units of Botox free," free consultations, and
  "20% off" promotions.

## Traditional dermatologists and plastic surgeons

- Many NYC dermatology and plastic-surgery practices offer the same injectables
  and lasers. They carry the strongest "real doctor" credibility, but are often
  perceived as more clinical, more expensive, and less experience-designed than
  a modern medspa.

## Where competitors cluster

- **Authority and expertise** — "largest," "most experienced," surgeon-founded,
  decades in practice. This claim is owned by default by the oldest and largest
  players.
- **Price promotions** — free units, free consultations, percentage-off offers —
  used aggressively across much of the field.
- **"Natural results"** — claimed explicitly by multiple competitors.
- The category's Meta / Instagram advertising is saturated with before/after
  imagery and discount offers.
