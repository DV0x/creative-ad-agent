# Founder Intake — Ever/Body

Notes from the intake call with the founder / marketing lead. Not every question
was answered.

- **Goal:** "We want more new patients through the door — first-time bookings —
  from our NYC clinics. Our existing patients rebook fine; the gap is reaching
  new people who've never been to us."

- **Conversion event:** a booked first appointment (a new patient). The
  "20% off your first visit" offer is the standard new-patient hook.

- **Budget:** about $8,000 / month for paid social (Meta), to start, across the
  NYC market.

- **Ad history:** "We've boosted Instagram posts — before/afters and the
  first-visit offer. Nothing structured, no real testing. We don't have a
  reliable cost-per-booking figure."

- **Unit economics:** *not provided.* The founder did not give an average
  first-visit ticket value or a patient lifetime value.

- **Creative assets:** real clinic and provider photography is available, and
  the team can shoot more.
