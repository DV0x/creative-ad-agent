# Founder Intake — Verbis Edu

Notes from the intake call with the founder.

- **Goal:** "We want more study-abroad leads — students who will go through our
  overseas-education service: university selection, applications, visa. That is
  the business we want to grow. And we want them from across India online, not
  only walk-ins to the Bangalore centre."

- **Conversion event:** a submitted "Check Eligibility" form on /apply, leading
  to a booked 30-minute counselling call.

- **Budget:** about ₹80,000 / month for Meta ads, to start.

- **Ad history:** "We've boosted Facebook posts — student score results, admit
  news, festival greetings. Mostly local reach. No structured testing, and we
  don't track cost-per-lead properly."

- **Where students come from today:** "Honestly, most of our students walk in or
  come by referral and word of mouth in Bangalore — and a lot of them start with
  our IELTS coaching. The online study-abroad enquiries are newer for us."

- **Unit economics:** not given a firm figure. The founder notes the study-
  abroad service is far higher-value per student than coaching, but did not
  state a per-student value or what an acceptable cost-per-lead would be.

- **Creative assets:** photos of trainers, the centres, and student results /
  admit letters are available.
