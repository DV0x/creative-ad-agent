# Brand Research — Verbis Edu

**URL:** https://www.verbisedu.com
**Category:** Education — English-test-prep coaching + overseas-education ("study abroad") consultancy
**Market:** India — Bangalore-based; serves students aiming to study/work abroad
**Researched:** 2026-05-19

## What the brand is

Verbis Edu is a Bangalore education business, established **2004** (around 20
years operating). It does two related things:

1. **Test-prep coaching** — classroom and online training for English-
   proficiency and other exams: IELTS, TOEFL, PTE, OET (GRE and GMAT are also
   listed). For example, a 60-hour IELTS program at roughly ₹8,000.
2. **Overseas-education ("study abroad") consultancy** — university selection,
   application support, visa guidance and "work abroad" guidance, across the UK,
   Canada, US, Australia, Germany, Ireland, New Zealand and Singapore.

It operates from Bangalore (a Mathikere Extension centre; a JP Nagar centre is
also listed) and cites a presence in Rajasthan. It runs both online and
in-person.

## How the brand describes itself

The website leads with the **study-abroad consultancy** identity. Taglines:
"Opening Doors to Global Opportunities," "Learn. Achieve. Succeed Abroad." The
tone is aspirational, mentorship-led and personal.

Headline claims on the site and the application funnel:

- "2,400+ students placed"
- "150+ partner universities"
- "95% visa success rate"
- "Proven Success Rate"; alumni at "leading global universities"
- Faculty described as "certified professionals with years of experience"

## The conversion funnel

The site's primary funnel is the **/apply "Check Eligibility"** flow: a short
multi-step form ("Free · 60 seconds · No spam") that asks which country the
student wants to study in, then collects profile and education details. It
promises (1) an eligibility assessment against the partner-university network,
and (2) a booked **30-minute consultation call** with a counsellor — "No hard
sell — just honest guidance." The conversion event is this lead: an eligibility-
form submission leading to a booked consultation call.

## Reputation and proof

- Verbis Edu has a real local review footprint: a **Justdial listing with about
  435 ratings**, described there as "top-rated," plus listings on Collegedunia
  and UrbanPro, and an active Facebook and LinkedIn presence.
- The substance of those reviews is overwhelmingly about the **test-prep
  coaching** — students crediting Verbis Edu, and named trainers (especially
  Parthasarathy Bhaskara and Vinutha), with achieving their target IELTS / PTE /
  OET scores, with good study material and approachable staff.
- The five testimonials on the website name the same trainers.
- What is **not** visible: independent, third-party verification of the
  *study-abroad outcomes*. The "2,400+ placed" and "95% visa success rate"
  figures are self-stated; there is no third-party review base specifically
  about placement or visa results the way there is for the coaching.

## The market

The Indian overseas-education consultancy market is large and growing, and
**crowded with big, well-funded national players** — IDP Education (50+ years,
global), Leverage Edu, AECC Global, Yocket, Leap Scholar — many of them
venture-backed, technology-driven, operating nationally with large scholarship
programs and AI shortlisting tools. See `competitors.md`.

The category also carries a serious, well-documented **trust problem** in India:
searches around study-abroad consultants surface heavy "scam warning," "fraud
agents," "red flags for parents" and "say no to consultants" content.
Prospective students and parents are widely advised to distrust consultants and
verify everything.

Test-prep coaching, by contrast, is a more commoditized but more
straightforwardly trusted local service — judged plainly on score outcomes.

## Visual reality

An education / service brand — the "product" is people and outcomes: trainers,
counsellors, classrooms, and student results (score reports, university admits,
visa approvals). The website is clean and modern. Real assets available would be
trainers, counsellors, students, the centres, and result / admit documentation.
