# Competitor Research — Indian Overseas-Education Consultancy & Test-Prep

The brand under research is Verbis Edu (see `research.md`), a Bangalore
test-prep and study-abroad consultancy.

## National study-abroad consultancy players

- **IDP Education** — global leader, 50+ years, operates as a placement agent
  for partner universities, and is a co-owner of the IELTS test. Vast scale and
  brand trust.
- **Leverage Edu** — venture-backed and technology-driven; end-to-end (test
  prep, application tracking, visa, accommodation), AI course-matching, and one
  of India's largest merit-scholarship programs.
- **AECC Global** — multi-country; positions explicitly on "transparent
  processes" and "ethical consulting"; strong Australia / New Zealand
  partnerships.
- **Yocket** — began as a student-community platform, now a fast-growing
  consultancy; technology-driven (budget tools, university shortlisting, SOP
  guidance, peer networking); popular for US MS / STEM applicants.
- **Leap Scholar**, SIEC, UniScholars, and many regional consultancies.

## Test-prep / coaching competitors

- Numerous IELTS / PTE / TOEFL coaching centres compete locally in Bangalore
  (Justdial alone lists many "top IELTS coaching" centres). Test prep is a
  crowded, locally-fought service, judged on score outcomes.

## Where competitors cluster

- The big consultancies advertise **scale and trust signals** — years in
  business, students placed, partner-university counts, scholarship programs,
  and "transparent / ethical" framing.
- Several lean on **technology** — AI shortlisting, budgeting tools, application
  tracking platforms.
- **Free or low-cost consultancy** is common (consultancies are paid commissions
  by universities), so "free counselling" is not itself a differentiator.
- The category advertises hard on trust precisely because the category's
  *reputation* is poor — scam-awareness content is everywhere a prospective
  student or parent looks.
