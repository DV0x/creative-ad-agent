# Brand Research — Bearaby

**URL:** https://bearaby.com
**Category:** Direct-to-consumer home wellness / weighted blankets
**Market:** Primarily United States, national (D2C, plus some retail)
**Researched:** 2026-05-19

## What the brand is

Bearaby is a D2C home-wellness brand best known for the **Napper** — a knitted,
"filler-free" weighted blanket. The weight comes from densely looped organic-
cotton yarn, not pellets or glass beads. Founded around 2018. It has since
extended into sheets, pillows (the Knot Pillow, body pillows), sleep masks, and
heated products (neck wraps, lap pads). 2024 revenue was about $22.5M.

Pricing: weighted blankets $169–$299 (Cotton Napper from $199, the TENCEL "Tree
Napper" from $249, Queen $299); Knot Pillow from $39; body pillow $149; sheet
sets from $269; sleep masks $55–$89.

## How the brand describes itself

Self-description: "Sustainable, Breathable & Filler-free Home Wellness Brand."
Tagline: "Designed for Rest, Backed by Science." The positioning rests on three
pillars:

- **Sustainability** — organic cotton, Fairtrade TENCEL, bamboo, "naturally
  antimicrobial" materials.
- **The knitted, filler-free design** — a distinctive chunky-knit object that is
  breathable and does not overheat, positioned explicitly against pellet-filled
  blankets.
- **Science-backed rest** — "85% report better sleep," "60% feel less anxious"
  (figures from an internal Bearaby customer survey).

The tone is warm, calm, and design-forward. The blanket is presented as a
beautiful object as much as a sleep aid — part of a "rest culture."

## The offer / conversion

Conversion event: an online purchase on bearaby.com. A weighted blanket is a
considered purchase ($199+), usually researched, and frequently bought as a
gift.

## Who buys — and why

Weighted blankets are bought by a strikingly wide range of people, for genuinely
different reasons:

- People with **anxiety / stress** — the deep-pressure calming effect; reviews
  also mention "touch starvation."
- People with **insomnia / poor sleep** — falling and staying asleep.
- **Hot sleepers** — Bearaby's breathable knit specifically wins people who
  found pellet blankets too hot; a warm-months use case.
- **Neurodivergent households** — weighted blankets are widely used for autism,
  ADHD and sensory needs (true of the category; Bearaby does not market to this
  explicitly, but is bought for it).
- People with **restless legs / chronic pain**.
- **Gift buyers** — a common occasion purchase.
- **Design-led buyers** — people who want the chunky-knit object for the look as
  much as the function.

Reviews consistently praise the even calming pressure, the breathability versus
pellet blankets, and the look. Recurring complaints: hard to clean, heavy and
awkward to move, the knit loops can snag (especially around pets), some sizes
"run small," and some buyers find cotton less soft or less warm than they
expected.

## The market

The therapeutic weighted-blanket market was about $1.2B in 2024, growing ~8.5%
a year. It is crowded. Demand has a seasonal shape — a gifting peak around the
holidays and a cozy-season skew to autumn/winter, with a breathable-blanket
counter-season for hot sleepers in summer. Competitors are detailed in
`competitors.md`.

## Visual reality

A visual-first product: the chunky knit is highly distinctive and photogenic —
the look is itself an asset. Bearaby's imagery is warm, natural-light and
lifestyle-led (the blanket draped over a person napping, on a sofa) in soft
earthy tones. Real product and lifestyle photography is abundant.
