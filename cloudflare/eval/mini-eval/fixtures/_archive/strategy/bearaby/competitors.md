# Competitor Research — Weighted Blankets / Home Wellness

The brand under research is Bearaby (see `research.md`). The market is D2C
weighted blankets, primarily the US.

## Gravity Blanket

- The category pioneer — launched via a record-setting Kickstarter in 2017, the
  brand that drove the modern weighted-blanket trend. 2024 revenue ~$55M,
  roughly 2.5× Bearaby.
- Pellet-filled (glass beads). Product line: Gravity Classic, Gravity Cooling,
  Gravity Kids.
- Positioning: the original and best-known name; broad "stress and sleep"
  messaging.

## The wider field

- Many competitors: SensaCalm, Mosaic, Layla Sleep, Calm Blanket, Baloo Living,
  Harkla, Luna, YnM, and others.
- YnM and similar Amazon-native brands are high-volume and low-price — they
  anchor a large mass-market tier.
- Harkla and SensaCalm lean explicitly into the sensory / autism / special-needs
  use case.
- Mattress and bedding brands (e.g. Layla) carry weighted blankets as a line
  extension.

## Where competitors cluster

- **"Stress and sleep"** generic wellness messaging — used broadly across the
  field.
- **Price** — a large low-cost tier (YnM and Amazon-native brands) anchors the
  category cheap.
- **The sensory / special-needs** use case — held more explicitly by Harkla and
  SensaCalm.
- Most competitors are **pellet-filled**; the knitted, filler-free construction
  is distinctive to Bearaby (and a few imitators).
- The category's Meta / Instagram advertising leans heavily on "calm," cozy
  lifestyle imagery and seasonal / gifting offers.
