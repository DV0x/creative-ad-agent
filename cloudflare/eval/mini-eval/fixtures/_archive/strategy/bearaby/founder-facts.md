# Founder Intake — Bearaby

Notes from the intake call with the founder / growth lead.

- **Goal:** "We've grown on PR, organic social, design press and retail. We're
  now putting real money into Meta cold acquisition — reaching people who have
  never heard of us — and we need that channel to pay."

- **Conversion event:** an online purchase on bearaby.com (the Napper weighted
  blanket is the lead product).

- **The open question, in the founder's words:** "Our blanket genuinely sells to
  very different people — the anxious, people who can't sleep, hot sleepers,
  parents buying for a kid who needs the sensory pressure, people buying it as a
  gift, people who just want the look in their living room. Organically they all
  find their own way to us. But for *paid cold* I honestly don't know which of
  them we should put the budget behind. We can't speak to all of them in one ad."

- **Budget:** about $25,000 / month for Meta, to start.

- **Ad history:** "We've boosted our best organic posts — the cozy lifestyle
  shots. No structured audience or message testing."

- **Unit economics:** average order value ~$210. The founder believes repeat
  purchases and gifting drive a meaningful share of revenue but does not have a
  firm lifetime-value figure.

- **Creative assets:** extensive lifestyle and product photography; able to
  shoot more.
