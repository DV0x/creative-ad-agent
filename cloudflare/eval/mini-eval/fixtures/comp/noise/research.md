# Brand Research — Noise

**URL:** https://www.gonoise.com
**Category:** Direct-to-consumer wearables & audio — smartwatches, smart rings, earbuds
**Market:** India, national
**Researched:** 2026-05-19

## What the brand is

Noise is an Indian D2C consumer-electronics brand — smartwatches (the ColorFit
range), a smart ring (the Luna Ring), and wireless audio (earbuds). It is
**India's #1 smartwatch brand by market share — about 26.6%** — ahead of boAt
(~14.1%) and Fire-Boltt (~9.7%). It built its position as a **pioneer of the
affordable smartwatch**: bringing smart features to accessible price points,
with smartwatches largely in the sub-₹5,000 range. Tagline: "Designed to Elevate
Every Part of Your Day."

## How the brand describes itself

Noise positions on accessible, design-led, feature-rich wearables for everyday
life. Recently it has been pushing **upmarket and into newer categories**: the
Luna Ring (a smart ring, a Red Dot Award winner) and the ColorFit Pro 6 series,
positioned as a "Pro for Pros" — AI features, a professional framing, shown at
CES 2025. So the public face is shifting from "affordable smartwatch for
everyone" toward "premium, AI-enabled, higher-value wearables."

## The offer / conversion

Conversion event: an online purchase (on gonoise.com and on marketplaces). A
smartwatch is a modest-ticket, moderately-considered purchase; the Luna Ring and
premium watches are higher-ticket and more considered.

## The market — and the pressure on it

This is the critical context. The Indian wearables market is **contracting**:
total wearables shipments fell ~4% in 2025 to ~114 million units, and
**smartwatch shipments fell ~17.6%**. The decline is expected to continue
through 2026. At the same time the category is in a **price war** — newer and
unknown brands have undercut the leaders with "dirt-cheap" smartwatches,
commoditising the affordable segment Noise pioneered. Industry analysts note
brands are responding by "focusing on higher-value products" — which is exactly
what Noise's Luna Ring and Pro-6 upmarket move is.

So Noise leads a category that is simultaneously **shrinking, commoditised, and
price-warred** — and it is trying to move up and out (rings, premium watches)
while still being known, by most buyers, as the affordable-watch brand.

## Visual reality

A visual-first product — the watches, the ring, finishes, displays and on-wrist
styling are the product. Noise has a large, polished creative library and a
strong, recognisable brand identity.

## Media cost reality (approximate — varies by geography and targeting)

*Approximate industry figures, not the brand's own confirmed numbers — treat as
a low-confidence range. The founder also supplied actual figures (see
`founder-facts.md`), which take precedence over these.*

- Indian Meta CPM is broadly ₹50–200 for feed, often ₹50–90, lower for Reels —
  roughly an order of magnitude below Western markets.
- Indian D2C electronics cost-per-acquisition varies widely with price point and
  creative; for accessible-priced wearables a cold-purchase CPA in the few-
  hundreds-of-rupees range is plausible, materially higher for premium pieces.
- Competition (see `competitors.md`): the category's Meta advertising is
  **heavily saturated** — Noise, boAt, Fire-Boltt and many challengers all
  advertise hard — which pushes CPMs in this category toward the upper end.
