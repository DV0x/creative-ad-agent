# Founder Intake — Noise

Notes from the intake call with the growth / performance-marketing lead.

- **Goal:** "We want to grow online purchases of our newer, higher-value
  products — the Luna Ring and the premium ColorFit Pro watches — from new
  customers via Meta. The affordable-watch base largely sells itself; the
  growth question is the move upmarket."

- **Conversion event:** an online purchase.

- **Budget:** about ₹30,00,000/month for Meta, for this initiative.

- **Ad history:** "We run Meta heavily and continuously, and we track it well.
  Our blended cold-acquisition CPA on the affordable smartwatches runs roughly
  **₹350–500**. On the premium pieces — the Luna Ring, the Pro watches — it is
  materially higher and we are still finding the level; early reads put it
  around **₹1,200–1,800**, and that is the number we want this work to improve."

- **Unit economics:** the premium pieces carry healthier margins than the
  affordable watches; the team is comfortable that a premium CPA in the
  low-four-figures of rupees can work if it holds.

- **Creative assets:** a large professional creative and video library.
