# Founder Intake — Arjun Infra

Notes from the intake call with the founder.

- **Goal:** "We want more genuine enquiries and site visits for our villa and
  apartment projects in Ongole — buyers who are serious, not just browsing."

- **Conversion event:** a qualified enquiry / booked site visit (via the website
  form, a phone call, or a brochure download).

- **Budget:** about ₹60,000/month for Meta ads, to start.

- **Ad history:** "We've boosted Facebook posts — photos of completed villas,
  festival greetings, project updates. Local reach. No structured testing, and
  we don't track a cost per enquiry."

- **Where buyers come from today:** "Mostly word of mouth in and around Ongole,
  referrals from past buyers, and some walk-ins after a hoarding or a boosted
  post. We know a lot of buyers are people from this area who are settled in
  Hyderabad or abroad — but we've never targeted them deliberately."

- **Unit economics:** not given a firm figure. A villa or apartment sale is
  high-value, but the founder did not state an average ticket size or what an
  acceptable cost-per-enquiry would be.

- **Creative assets:** project photos, elevation renders, and completed-villa
  walkthroughs are available.
