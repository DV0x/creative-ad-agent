# Founder Intake — DailyObjects

Notes from the intake call with the founder / growth lead.

- **Goal:** "We want to grow online purchases from new customers — people who
  don't already know DailyObjects — through Meta."

- **Conversion event:** an online purchase on dailyobjects.com.

- **Budget:** about ₹5,00,000/month for Meta ads.

- **Ad history:** "We run Meta regularly — mostly product-catalogue ads and
  boosted design drops. We watch blended ROAS at the account level, but we
  don't have a clean cost-per-acquisition broken out by product line, or by
  new versus returning customer."

- **Unit economics:** average order value is in the mid-hundreds of rupees for
  cases and higher for bags; a firm blended new-customer CPA was not provided.

- **Creative assets:** extensive product and lifestyle photography, and a strong
  in-house design identity.
