# Competitor Research — Indian Wearables (Smartwatches / Smart Rings / Audio)

The brand under research is Noise (see `research.md`), India's #1 smartwatch
brand.

## Direct competitors

- **boAt** — ~14.1% smartwatch share (#2). Strong in audio; positions rugged
  smartwatches for youth, and affordable smart rings. A large, loud consumer
  brand.
- **Fire-Boltt** — ~9.7% (#3). The aggressive price challenger — consistently
  undercuts on price while matching features.
- **A long tail of newer / unknown brands** — these have launched "dirt-cheap"
  smartwatches that undercut the established players and commoditised the
  affordable segment.
- In smart rings specifically, global players (e.g. Oura-type rings) and a
  growing set of entrants compete with the Luna Ring.

## Where competitors cluster

- **Price** — the dominant battlefield; the category is in a documented price
  war, with challengers driving the affordable segment toward the floor.
- **Features-per-rupee** — more sensors, AI features, bigger and brighter
  displays at the same or a lower price.
- **Youth / lifestyle / rugged** branding (boAt especially).
- The category's Meta and Instagram advertising is intense and saturated — every
  major brand spends heavily, with frequent product launches and offers.
