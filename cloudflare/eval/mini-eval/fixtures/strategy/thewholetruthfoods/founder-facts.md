# Founder Intake — The Whole Truth Foods

Rough notes from an intake call with the founder.

- Site is thewholetruthfoods.com — "go through the site, you'll get what we're
  about. We're a clean-label food brand — no bullshit, everything's on the
  label, nothing hidden."

- Range across a few categories. Didn't list it all out — "it's all on the
  site."

- This campaign is about the **protein powder / whey** line — that's what we
  want to push right now.

- Goal: new customers buying off Instagram/Facebook ads, straight to the
  website. First-time buyers — not our existing fans or repeat folks.
  Spending roughly ₹2–5 lakh a month on Meta.

- Honest about the big unknown: "we don't really know who actually buys the
  whey cold off an ad. We have a sense of our audience overall, but not who
  converts when they've never heard of us before." Wants the test to help
  figure that out.

- Couldn't give clean numbers on CPA or what a customer is worth — "I'd have
  to pull that from the team."

- Uploaded a product photo of the whey to work with (in reference-images/).
