# Competitor Research — Indian D2C Tech & Lifestyle Accessories

The brand under research is DailyObjects (see `research.md`).

## The competitive field

- **The cheap-case mass market** — a vast supply of low-cost phone cases on
  Amazon and Flipkart, and countless unbranded sellers. This sets a very low
  price anchor; most phone-case buyers in India default here.
- **International premium case brands** — e.g. Spigen and similar — available in
  India, competing on protection, drop-rating and brand.
- **Other Indian D2C design / accessory brands** — a growing set of design-led
  players in cases, bags and lifestyle accessories.
- For bags and wallets specifically, DailyObjects also competes with established
  leather and lifestyle brands.

## Where competitors cluster

- **Price** — the dominant axis at the bottom of the market; cheap cases are
  everywhere and set the anchor.
- **Protection / ruggedness** — the claim international case brands lean on.
- **Design and aesthetics** — a more crowded claim than it once was; many D2C
  brands now compete on "design-led."
- The category's Meta / Instagram advertising is heavy on product-on-aesthetic-
  background imagery, new-design drops, and festive / gifting offers.
