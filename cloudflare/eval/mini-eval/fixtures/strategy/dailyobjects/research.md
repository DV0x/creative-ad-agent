# Brand Research — DailyObjects

**URL:** https://www.dailyobjects.com
**Category:** Direct-to-consumer tech & lifestyle accessories
**Market:** India, national (D2C; also a corporate-gifting line)
**Researched:** 2026-05-19

## What the brand is

DailyObjects is an Indian D2C brand selling design-led tech and lifestyle
accessories — phone cases and covers (heavily iPhone / Apple-focused), MagSafe
accessories, Apple Watch bands and straps, wallets, bags, desk organisers,
notebooks, and travel accessories. Based in Gurugram; founded by Pankaj and
Saurav. It describes its products as "crafted with care by 1000+ Indian artisans
and makers," using "global-quality materials" but "built locally." It also runs
a corporate-gifting (B2B) line.

## How the brand describes itself

Self-description: "India's most-loved brand for iPhone cases, MagSafe
accessories, Apple Watch bands, wallets and bags." Positioning is design-forward
— minimalism, curated "collections," "new arrivals," trend-aware. The marketing
voice is casual and lifestyle-led ("Slide into order"). Pricing spans roughly
₹500 to ₹5,000+, a mid-market-to-premium position. Target: design-conscious
professionals and tech enthusiasts, especially Apple users; and gift-buyers.

## The offer / conversion

Conversion event: an online purchase on dailyobjects.com. A phone case is a
low-deliberation, modest-ticket purchase; bags and premium pieces are more
considered.

## Reputation and proof

Reputation is **genuinely split**:

- The DailyObjects app holds a strong 4.7+ rating from 21,000+ users; many
  customers praise the design, the minimalism, and the attention to detail.
- But independent review platforms (Trustpilot and others) carry a recurring
  pattern of complaints — substandard product quality, poor customer service,
  and delivery issues. Recurring specifics: **colour fading after a wash, prints
  peeling after contact with water.** So the design-led promise is, for a
  meaningful slice of buyers, not matched by durability or after-sales care.

## The market

The Indian D2C phone-case and tech-accessory market is large, fast-moving, and
extremely crowded at the bottom: a vast supply of cheap cases on Amazon and
Flipkart sets a very low price anchor. Demand is steady, with gifting peaks
(the festive season, Raksha Bandhan) and spikes around new iPhone launches.

## Visual reality

A visual-first product — the case designs, materials, colours and the desk /
lifestyle styling are the product. DailyObjects has a strong, recognisable
design identity and abundant product and lifestyle photography.

## Media cost reality (approximate — varies by geography and targeting)

*Approximate industry figures, not the brand's own confirmed numbers — treat as
a low-confidence range.*

- Indian Meta CPM is broadly ₹50–200 for feed, often ₹50–90, lower for Reels —
  roughly an order of magnitude below Western markets.
- Indian D2C e-commerce cost-per-acquisition varies widely with product price
  and creative — broadly an India e-commerce result cost of roughly **₹400–2,000+**,
  depending heavily on the product and the audience.
- A modest-ticket impulse product (a phone case) and a considered one (a bag)
  behave very differently on cost-per-purchase and on volume.
- Competition (see `competitors.md`): the category's Meta advertising is
  crowded, which pushes CPMs toward the upper end.
