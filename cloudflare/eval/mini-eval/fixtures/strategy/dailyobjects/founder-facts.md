# Founder Intake — DailyObjects

Rough notes from a quick call with the founder. They were busy and didn't
go into much detail.

- Site is dailyobjects.com — "just look at the site, you'll get what we do."

- They make everyday-carry and tech stuff — phone cases, some bags, desk
  things. Wide range. Didn't list it all out.

- Wants more sales coming from Instagram and Facebook ads, straight to the
  website. Spends a decent amount every month on it already.

- Main frustration: "our ads look like everyone else's — nice product on a
  background. We don't stand out. We want creative that actually feels like
  us and makes people stop."

- Mentioned cases are what most people buy first, and they're trying to
  push bags more this year. Didn't give numbers.

- Couldn't say what a customer is worth or what they pay per sale — "I'd
  have to check with the team."

- Uploaded a couple of product photos to work with (in reference-images/).
