# Brand Research — Arjun Infra

**URL:** https://arjuninfra.com
**Category:** Real estate — residential property developer
**Market:** Ongole, Andhra Pradesh, India (a tier-3 city)
**Researched:** 2026-05-19

## What the brand is

Arjun Infra is a residential real-estate developer based in Ongole, a tier-3
city in Andhra Pradesh. Established 2016. It builds gated-community villas (3 and
4 BHK), high-rise apartments (3–4 BHK), and ultra-luxury villas — its current
project is "Uma Panoruma" (ultra-luxury villas), with an upcoming project,
"Skyla," described as contemporary luxury. Its office is at Market Yard, Ongole.
It is a small, owner-run local operation — the public contact point is a
personal mobile number and a Gmail address.

## How the brand describes itself

Self-description: "Icon of Ongole." Mission language: "building trusted
communities — connecting families, spaces, and lifestyles through quality real
estate development." It calls itself a "Trusted Real Estate Developer." Three
stated pillars: Build Quality, Fine Finish, Smart Design. It stresses
"transparent processes" and "thoughtfully planned" communities. The site shows a
self-rated 4.8 stars. Tone: aspirational but professional — family, trust, and
modern living.

## The offer / conversion

A buyer enquires via an on-site contact/enquiry form, an e-brochure download, a
phone call, or a booked site visit. The conversion event is a qualified enquiry
/ booked site visit — a lead. The product itself (a villa or apartment) is a
very high-ticket, long-deliberation purchase: the developer needs relatively
few leads, but each genuine buyer is worth a great deal.

## Reputation and proof

Arjun Infra has a thin third-party footprint. No substantial independent review
base surfaced — no significant Google or JustDial review presence; the visible
testimonials sit on the company's own site (positive: thoughtful villa design,
quality construction, a smooth and transparent process, responsive staff). The
4.8-star figure is self-stated. For a stranger researching the developer online,
there is little independent proof to find — typical of a small local builder.

## The market — and who buys tier-3 AP property

Ongole is a tier-3 Andhra Pradesh city. Demand for gated villas and apartments
in a town this size comes from a few distinct sources: local Ongole families
upgrading their home; and — significant for coastal-AP towns — the **diaspora**:
people from the Ongole region working in Hyderabad, other Indian metros, the US
or the Gulf, who buy property back in their hometown (as an investment, for
parents, or for an eventual return). Real estate is a high-trust, high-
consideration purchase — buyers research a developer's track record and
completed projects heavily before they part with money.

## Media cost reality (approximate — varies sharply by geography)

*Approximate industry figures, not the developer's own confirmed numbers — treat
as a low-confidence range.*

- Indian Meta CPM is far below Western markets — broadly ₹50–200 for feed
  placements, often ₹50–90, and lower for Reels.
- Real-estate property-enquiry cost-per-lead in India runs broadly **₹300–1,200**,
  with tier-1 metros at the higher end (≈₹550–1,400). A **tier-3 city like
  Ongole, targeted locally and in Telugu, should sit well below the metro
  range** — geography moves this number heavily.
- Meta native lead-forms typically cost 30–50% less per lead than sending
  traffic to a landing page.
- Competition (see `competitors.md`): the local Ongole / coastal-AP builder ad
  space is far less saturated than metro real estate, which tends to keep costs
  toward the lower end.
