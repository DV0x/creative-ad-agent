# Founder Intake — AS-IT-IS Nutrition

Notes from the intake call with the founder.

## Brand identification

- **Brand name:** AS-IT-IS Nutrition
- **Website:** https://asitisnutrition.com/
- **Location / scope:** India. Pan-India e-commerce (own site + marketplaces).

## What the founder said

- **Goal:** "We want to grow first-time D2C purchases on our own website, especially for our whey protein. We sell a lot through Amazon, but the margins on our own site are better and we want more direct customers and repeat buyers. We also do well with peanut butter — that pulls in a slightly different crowd."

- **Conversion event:** an online purchase on `asitisnutrition.com` (checkout completion). Marketplace orders are outside Meta attribution and not the target.

- **Budget:** ₹3,00,000/month for Meta ads, with room to scale if it works.

- **Targeting brief:** "Fitness-focused Indians — gym-goers, people serious about their protein intake, value-conscious buyers who read the label and don't want to pay for fancy packaging or marketing. We're known for being no-nonsense and honestly priced."

- **Ad history:** "We've run Meta ads on and off. Engagement is best on the 'no additives, honest pricing' angle. First-time CPL has been roughly ₹150–₹350; AOV around ₹700–₹1,200 depending on what they buy."

- **Where buyers come from today:** Marketplaces (Amazon, etc.) take the majority of revenue; own-site is a smaller but higher-margin channel we want to grow. A loyal repeat base on the supplements.

- **Unit economics:** AOV ₹700–₹1,200 first order. LTV not shared exactly, but "repeat purchase on consumables is strong — protein and peanut butter are restock items."

- **Creative assets:** Existing product photography and some founder-led content. None uploaded to this workspace for this engagement.
