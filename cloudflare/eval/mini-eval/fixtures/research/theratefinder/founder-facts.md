# Founder Intake — TheRateFinder

Notes from the intake form submitted by the brand.

## Brand identification

- **Brand name:** TheRateFinder
- **Website:** https://theratefinder.ca
- **Location / scope:** Canada. National — positions itself as a place for Canadians to find and compare mortgage rates.

## What the brand said

- **Goal:** "We want more mortgage leads. We're a lending hub — borrowers come to find their best mortgage rate, and we connect them to the right lender. We need a steady flow of qualified borrower enquiries."

- **Conversion event:** a mortgage lead — a borrower submits their details through the site's rate-enquiry / application form.

- **Budget:** CAD $8,000/month for Meta ads to start, willing to scale if the leads convert.

- **Targeting brief:** "Canadians shopping for a mortgage — first-time buyers, people up for renewal, anyone who wants a better rate than their bank is offering. Nationwide, English-speaking primarily."

- **Ad history:** "We started running Meta ads recently — only a few weeks in. Still figuring out what works. No real performance baseline yet."

- **Where leads come from today:** "Mostly the new ad campaigns. We don't have much organic yet — we're a newer platform."

- **Unit economics:** Not provided. (No cost-per-lead history, no stated acceptable CPL.)

- **Creative assets:** None uploaded to this workspace for this engagement.
