# Founder Intake — Arjun Infra

Notes from the intake call with the founder.

## Brand identification

- **Brand name:** Arjun Infra
- **Location:** Ongole, Prakasam district, Andhra Pradesh, India
- **Website:** the founder did not share a dedicated brand website. They
  mentioned the brand appears on JustDial and 99acres listings. A search
  surface they referenced:
  `https://www.justdial.com/Ongole/Arjun-Infra-Opposite-Ongole-Market-YardBeside-Hp-Petrol-Bunk-Dharavari-Thota-Road/9999P8592-8592-230209185653-C8V5_BZDET`

## What the founder said

- **Goal:** "We want more genuine enquiries and site visits for our villa
  and apartment projects in Ongole — buyers who are serious, not just
  browsing."

- **Conversion event:** a qualified enquiry or booked site visit (via
  phone call, JustDial enquiry form, or a brochure WhatsApp request).

- **Budget:** about ₹60,000/month for Meta ads, to start.

- **Targeting brief:** "Target Ongole and surrounding areas. We also know
  a lot of buyers are people from this part of Andhra who are now settled
  in Hyderabad, Bangalore, or abroad (US, UAE) — they want to invest back
  home or buy something for their parents. We've never targeted them
  deliberately but we should."

- **Ad history:** "We've boosted Facebook posts — photos of completed
  villas, festival greetings, project updates. Local reach. No structured
  testing, and we don't track a cost per enquiry."

- **Where buyers come from today:** "Mostly word of mouth in and around
  Ongole, referrals from past buyers, and some walk-ins after a hoarding
  or a boosted post."

- **Unit economics:** not given a firm figure. A villa or apartment sale
  is high-value (ranges from low tens of lakhs to a crore-plus), but the
  founder did not state an average ticket size or what an acceptable
  cost-per-enquiry would be.

- **Creative assets:** project photos, elevation renders, and
  completed-villa walkthrough videos are available "if needed" — but the
  founder did not upload any to the workspace for this engagement.
