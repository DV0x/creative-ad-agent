# Founder Intake — Verbis Education

Rough notes from an intake call with the founder.

- Site is verbisedu.com — "we're a study-abroad education consultancy. We help
  students figure out which universities abroad they're actually eligible for,
  sort out their applications, and get them through the visa. Go through the
  site, you'll get what we do."

- This campaign is about the **free counselling lead** — the /apply page
  (verbisedu.com/apply). A student fills a short eligibility form and books a
  free counselling call with an advisor. "That free call is the thing we want
  more of — that's where we actually convert people."

- Goal: new leads from Instagram/Facebook ads — cold students (and sometimes
  parents) who've never heard of us, landing on /apply and booking the free
  counselling call. Not our existing pipeline, not referrals.

- Audience: students who want to study abroad (UK, Canada, US, Australia,
  Germany, Ireland, and the like). "Honestly we're not sure who actually books
  the free call off a cold ad — the ambitious final-year student? the parent
  paying for it? the working professional doing a master's later? That's what
  we'd want a test to tell us."

- Selling points the founder rattled off (please verify — don't take our word):
  "we've got a really high visa success rate, we partner with a lot of
  universities abroad, and we've placed thousands of students. Students tell us
  they like that we're honest about where they can actually get in, instead of
  overpromising."

- Couldn't give clean numbers on cost-per-lead or what a booked call is worth —
  "I'd have to pull that from the team."

- No product to photograph — it's a service. Didn't upload any images.
