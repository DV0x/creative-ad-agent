# Founder Intake — The Whole Truth Foods

Notes from the intake call with the founder.

## Brand identification

- **Brand name:** The Whole Truth Foods (often abbreviated "Whole Truth" by customers)
- **Website:** https://thewholetruthfoods.com
- **Location / scope:** Headquartered in Mumbai, India. Pan-India e-commerce (own site + Amazon + select retail chains).

## What the founder said

- **Goal:** "We want to grow first-time D2C purchases on our own site for our chocolate range and protein bars. We do well on Amazon, but margins are better on the own-site channel, and we want a base of repeat subscribers."

- **Conversion event:** an online purchase via `thewholetruthfoods.com` (checkout completion). Amazon orders fall outside Meta attribution and are not the target of this campaign.

- **Budget:** ₹4,00,000/month for Meta ads, with room to scale if results work.

- **Targeting brief:** "Pan-India, urban India tier-1 and tier-2 cities, English-speaking primarily. Our buyers index strongly on health-conscious millennials and Gen Z — gym-goers, parents who read ingredient labels, urban professionals replacing junk snacking. We've done some lookalikes off our own purchaser data and they work."

- **Ad history:** Active Meta advertiser since ~2020. Direct-response creatives focused on the "no junk" positioning (no added sugar, no preservatives, no artificial sweeteners). "We see strong engagement on ingredient-transparency content. First-time CPL on Meta has been in the ₹100–₹300 range; AOV ₹600–₹900 on first-time orders. Some seasonal swings."

- **Where buyers come from today:** Meta and Google for first-time D2C site purchases; Amazon takes a majority share of total revenue. Small but loyal subscriber base. Some sport partnerships (e.g., Indian Rugby Football Union announced as official protein partner in November 2025).

- **Unit economics:** AOV ₹600–₹900 first order. LTV "we don't share exact numbers" but described as "multi-purchase repeat economics that justify a healthy CPL on first order."

- **Creative assets:** Existing brand creative library is rich (product photography, motion graphics, founder-led content). None uploaded to this workspace for this engagement.
